# Nia's apartment: set image prompts

These prompts create the reference images for Nia's apartment. Each approved image becomes a Higgsfield environment element, the same way `Nia-Bedroom-Night` and `ChiChi-Living-Room-Night-v2` were made.

## How to make them consistent

1. **Generate the living room first** (Prompt 1). It sets the look of the whole apartment.
2. **Generate everything else with the approved living room attached as a reference image,** plus `Nia-Bedroom-Night` (`b6a4c5be…`), so the materials, colours and light carry across.
3. **Settings:** 16:9, highest resolution. Sets read better wide; the 9:16 framing happens in the video. Use a model that takes reference images, such as Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 4.5 or GPT Image 2.
4. **Generate 2–4 options of each** and pick one.
5. **Every prompt includes:** no people, no text anywhere, nothing written on any surface.

**The apartment's look, shared by every prompt:**
- **Style and palette:** Nia's high-rise city apartment, warm bohemian and lived-in, matching her bedroom. Cream, oatmeal, beige, sand and pale honey, with natural wood, rattan, jute and linen.
- **Plants and decor:** trailing pothos, dried pampas grass and a white paper lantern.
- **Feel:** cosy and slightly more chaotic than ChiChi's immaculate apartment, but tidy.
- **Light:** soft, warm MORNING sun through large windows.
