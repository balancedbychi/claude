# Social Video Prompt Library

Reusable image + motion prompts for GRWM, routines, lifestyle, and day-in-the-life content.
These follow formats that show up again and again across TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and AI-video
tutorials on YouTube. Swap the `[brackets]` for your own details.

---

## 1. How to use this library

**Workflow (keyframe → motion → edit):**
1. Generate the **image prompt** as a 9:16 still.
2. Animate that still with the **motion prompt** (image-to-video, 5–10s).
3. Stitch 4–8 clips, add trending audio or voiceover, captions, and post.

**Motion prompt formula:**
`[subject] + [one action] + [one camera move] + [lighting] + [mood/pace]`

Rules that keep AI motion clean:
- One action and one camera move per clip.
- Describe *slow* movement. Fast hand actions (makeup, pouring) distort easily.
- Keep faces medium or far, or crop to hands. Close faces drift between clips.
- Reuse the same **style anchor** in every prompt so a series looks like one brand.

### Style anchor (paste at the end of every prompt)

Pick one and stick with it for a whole series:

- **Clean girl / soft neutral:** `soft natural window light, warm neutral tones, beige and cream palette, airy and minimal, shallow depth of field, shot on iPhone, 9:16`
- **Film / nostalgic:** `35mm film look, subtle grain, golden hour, warm highlights, slightly faded blacks, 9:16`
- **Spa / calm wellness:** `diffused morning light, sage green and linen tones, steam and soft haze, serene and slow, 9:16`
- **Cozy night:** `warm lamp light, candle glow, deep amber and brown tones, soft shadows, intimate and quiet, 9:16`

### Character consistency

For a recurring "you" or brand persona, describe them the same way every time:
`[a woman in her late 20s, shoulder-length dark curly hair, gold hoop earrings, cream ribbed loungewear]`
Better still, make one reference image or character sheet and feed it into every generation.

## 8. Proven formats and hooks

Video structures that keep getting reused because they hold attention:

- **POV:** "POV: you finally started taking care of yourself"
- **Before → after:** chaos morning vs. calm morning, split or back-to-back
- **Numbered list:** "3 habits that changed my mornings" with one clip per habit
- **"Things that just make sense":** a quick montage of small aesthetic moments
- **Reset:** "Sunday reset" / "monthly reset" cleaning and planning montage
- **Romanticize:** "romanticizing my 9–5" with slow B-roll over mundane tasks
- **ASMR routine:** no music, just the sound of pouring, tapping, and brushing
- **Voiceover story:** calm B-roll under a personal story, e.g. "how I healed my relationship with food"

## 10. Quick template

Copy this for any new shot:

```
IMAGE:  [subject/character] [doing what] in [location], [key props], [style anchor]
MOTION: [one slow action], [one camera move: push-in / pull-back / orbit / track / static], [lighting change if any], calm pace
TEXT:   [hook line for on-screen text]
```
