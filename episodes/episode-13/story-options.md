## Option A · "Read Receipts" (recommended)

**Where / who:** the same kitchen, the same morning, seconds later. Nia and ChiChi only.
**Story:** They read the texts out loud.
- **DB has done the opposite of "penciled in".** He has cleared his whole Saturday and asks ChiChi to choose what they do. Her objection is gone, and she isn't sure she likes losing it.
- **Tay asks Nia to his business launch this weekend,** and to meet his people: the "more" ChiChi just told her to take seriously. Nia starts typing a joke and ChiChi takes the phone.
- Each makes the other reply honestly. ChiChi sends "Saturday." Nia sends "Yes."
**Button:** Nia's phone buzzes once more. Dorian. Nia doesn't say his name; ChiChi reads it off her face.
**Why this one:** it pays off Episode 12's cliffhanger straight away, and Nia gets the theme choice: she says yes to more, then the old pattern texts her. The set and both looks are already locked, so no new uploads or casting are needed. It's the cheapest option, about 500–550 credits for 5–6 clips.
**Risk:** two kitchen episodes back to back. The fix is to keep it short (about 60 seconds) and use it as a bridge into Episodes 14 and 15 (Saturday with DB, the launch with Tay).
