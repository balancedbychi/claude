## Logline

The morning after the phone call, Dorian turns up at Nia's door with a bouquet that cost more than her rent. She lets him in, and he explains the "miscommunication." Simone isn't a stranger. They've been on and off for a year and a half.

---

## Door logic (series rule 5)

Nia's front door **opens inward, into her apartment**. Seen from inside, the **hinges are on the LEFT** and the **handle is on the RIGHT**. She pulls the handle toward herself and the door swings in on its left-hand hinges. When she lets him in, she **steps back and swings the door wide**, and he walks in past her.

## Script

### SEGMENT 1 — "ARE YOU GOING TO KEEP ME OUTSIDE?" (0:00–0:20)
*INT. NIA'S APARTMENT, FRONT DOOR — MORNING, cutting between Nia inside and Dorian in the corridor*

*Soft morning light. A knock. Nia opens the door and stops dead. Dorian stands there holding the enormous bouquet.*

> **NIA**
> *(surprised)*
> …Dorian.

> **DORIAN**
> Morning. Can I come in?

> **NIA**
> Why should I let you in?

> **DORIAN**
> Come on. You're really going to keep me outside?

*A beat. Nia exhales, steps back and swings the door wide open. Dorian steps in past her, bouquet first.*

---

### SEGMENT 2 — "YOU." (0:20–0:50)
*INT. NIA'S LIVING ROOM — MORNING*

*Nia closes the door behind him and folds her arms. Dorian stands holding the flowers in the middle of her living room.*

> **NIA**
> What are we doing?

> **DORIAN**
> Look, I think there was just a bit of miscommunication.

> **NIA**
> Miscommunication? There was another girl at your place.

> **DORIAN**
> I shouldn't have told you to come over. I knew Simone was —

> **NIA**
> *(cutting in)*
> Simone.

> **DORIAN**
> Yeah. Simone.

> **NIA**
> Oh, so we're on a first-name basis now?

*Dorian laughs nervously.*

> **DORIAN**
> We've been dating about a year and a half. On and off.

*Nia is stunned. She can't believe what she's hearing.*

> **DORIAN**
> Look, I wasn't really expecting us to turn into anything. But I really like your vibe. I like how you make me feel.

> **NIA**
> And what's that supposed to mean to me?

> **DORIAN**
> I hope it means you feel the same way.

> **NIA**
> Dorian. What do you want?

> **DORIAN**
> You.

*CUT TO BLACK on his answer.* **END CARD:** *EXCLUSIVE with Nia and Chi*

- **Ending:** the episode ends on Dorian's word "You." with no reaction shot after it.
- **Spoken word count:** about 125 words in about 50 seconds.
