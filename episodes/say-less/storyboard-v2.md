## How the two problems get fixed

### 1. The duplicate Dorian
- **What went wrong:** in the old Segment 1, Dorian came from the casting-sheet look. In Segment 2 he came from your full-length upload. The model kept the Segment 1 Dorian (the reference video) and added a second one from the image.
- **The fix:** **Dorian comes from `Dorian-Full` in BOTH segments, from the very first frame.** So the Segment 1 we approve already shows the right Dorian, and Segment 2's reference video and image agree.
- Every prompt also gives a headcount ("exactly four main people: one Nia, one Tay, one Dorian, one Simone; never two of anyone"). Segment 2 starts with Dorian already in frame at the back with Simone, so nobody new walks in.

### 2. The collision angle
- **What went wrong:** the old prompt never said who was on which side of the screen, so the model guessed.
- **The fix:** the floor plan below locks screen sides from the first frame to the last. **Tay is ALWAYS on screen LEFT of Nia, and Dorian is ALWAYS on screen RIGHT of Nia.** That holds through every cut in both segments.
