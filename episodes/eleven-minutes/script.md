## Logline

The night after the phone call, Dorian turns up at Nia's door to explain in person. She won't let him in. He has one move left, and it's the one thing he has never done.

## Door logic (series rule 5)

Nia's front door **opens inward, into her apartment**. Seen from inside, the **hinges are on the LEFT** and the **handle is on the RIGHT**. She opens it by pulling the handle toward herself, so it swings in on the left-hand hinges. She keeps it only **half open**, her body in the gap, one hand on the edge of the door. **Dorian never crosses the threshold.**

*Same door. Then INT. NIA'S ENTRY — her back against the closed door*

> **DORIAN**
> She does now.

> **NIA**

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)** *(over her face, laid in during the edit)*
