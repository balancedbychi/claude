# EXCLUSIVE with Nia and Chi: "Day Eleven"

**Format:** scripted short, vertical 9:16
**Runtime:** about 80 seconds, in 6 clips (C1 to C6) of 12 to 15 seconds each
**Set:** ChiChi's living room at night (`ChiChi-Living-Room-Night-v2`), the one location for the whole episode
**Cast:** Nia · ChiChi · Narrator (voiceover only) · Simone (her text message only, never on screen)
**Where it sits:** eleven days after Dorian's house party in "The Caterer"

---

## Logline

Dorian texts every eleven days, and today is day eleven. Nia has sworn she won't reply. ChiChi has poured the wine to watch her try. The phone finally lights up, and it isn't Dorian.

---

## Voices

| Who | Voice | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| **NIA** | `Nia-Canon-Voice-v2` | Warm and low, with a British accent. Dry and deadpan. Quick, and a little too sure of herself. |
| **CHICHI** | `ChiChi-Canon-Voice-v1` | Unhurried and deadpan, warm, with calm authority. Never raises her voice. She already knows how this ends. |
| **NARRATOR** | To be cast from a preset; Maeve or Helena would suit | A rich, wry, intimate voice-of-God. Sparing: she speaks only between scenes and never over dialogue. |

## Script

### C1 — COLD OPEN (0:00–0:13)

*INT. CHICHI'S LIVING ROOM — NIGHT.*

*Wide shot, 35mm, eye level. The city glitters through the corner windows. ONE warm lamp is lit on the media console. The cream sectional runs the width of the room. ChiChi sits at its right end with her feet tucked under her. Nia is on the chaise. Two glasses of red wine stand on the round marble coffee table. Between them, Nia's phone lies FACE DOWN.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Some men call when they miss you.
> Some men call when they're bored.
> And some men… are on a schedule.

*Slow push toward the phone on the marble.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Exclusive.

**TITLE CARD:** *EXCLUSIVE — "Day Eleven"*

---

### C2 — THE RULE (0:13–0:27)

*Two-shot. Nia sits upright with her arms folded and her eyes off the phone, working at it.*

> **NIA**
> I'm not replying.

> **CHICHI**
> *(sipping)*
> Mm.

> **NIA**
> I mean it. Eleven days, Chi. He texts like it's a subscription.

> **CHICHI**
> And you keep renewing.

*Nia shoots her a look. ChiChi doesn't blink.*

---

### C3 — THE CLOCK (0:27–0:40)

*Close-up of Nia. Her eyes slide toward the phone, then away.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Eleven days since the party. Since the pot, the cousin, and the white dress she still hasn't gotten clean.

*Back to the two-shot.*

> **CHICHI**
> What time did he text last time?

> **NIA**
> *(instantly)*
> Nine-forty.

*Beat. She heard herself.*

> **NIA**
> …Roughly.

*ChiChi lifts her glass at Nia, saying nothing.*

---

### C4 — THE BUZZ (0:40–0:53)

*The lamp hums and the room goes still. Insert: the phone shudders against the marble, and a glow leaks out from under its edges.*

*Nia doesn't move. ChiChi doesn't move. Both of them are staring at it.*

> **CHICHI**
> *(evenly)*
> Don't.

> **NIA**
> I'm not.

*Her hand is already halfway across the table.*

---

### C5 — THE TEXT (0:53–1:07)

*Nia turns the phone over, and we push in over her shoulder.*

> **NIA**
> *(reading, flat)*
> It's not him.

> **CHICHI**
> Who is it?

> **NIA**
> Simone.

*Nia reads the message aloud, her voice getting quieter with each line.*

> **NIA**
> "Hiii! Brunch Saturday? Dorian's cooking! Bring Chi!" …Three exclamation marks.

*ChiChi sets her glass down very carefully.*

> **CHICHI**
> She's lovely.

> **NIA**
> She's *lovely.*

---

### C6 — TAG (1:07–1:20)

*Wide shot again: the two women, the city behind them, and the phone glowing face up now.*

> **CHICHI**
> So. Are you replying?

*Long beat. Nia picks up her wine.*

> **NIA**
> …What time's brunch?

*ChiChi closes her eyes and slowly shakes her head.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Day eleven. Same as always.
> *(beat)*
> She'll be there at ten.

*CUT TO BLACK.*

**END CARD:** *EXCLUSIVE with Nia and Chi*
