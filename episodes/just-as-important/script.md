## SEGMENT 2 (about 30 s): Always wanted one / Why it ended

*SAME, CONTINUOUS. DB settles, warmer, leaning in a little on his forearms.*

> **DB** `[DB-JAI]`
> If it helps, I'm in a similar boat. No children. And I have always wanted one.

*ChiChi's brows lift a fraction. That one landed.*

> **DB** `[DB-JAI]`
> But work always got in the way of my relationships. I want someone who understands my life is busy.

*Half a second: ChiChi's pleasant smile holds, then adjusts slightly. Her eyes flick once to his face-down phone and back.*

> **CHICHI** `[ChiChi-JAI]`
> Can I ask you something?

> **DB** `[DB-JAI]`
> Anything.

> **CHICHI** `[ChiChi-JAI]`
> Is that why your marriage ended?

*DB's smile fades. Not defensive, just honest. He looks down at the candle.*

> **DB** `[DB-JAI]`
> Yes. She wanted a lot of time. I couldn't give it. *(looking back up at her)* I've been working on that. I'm in therapy.

> **CHICHI** `[ChiChi-JAI]` *(surprised, a little impressed)*
> Therapy.

> **DB** `[DB-JAI]`
> It's helped. More than I expected.

*(End of Segment 2. Final line: DB's "It's helped. More than I expected.")*
