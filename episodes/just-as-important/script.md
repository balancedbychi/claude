## THE STORY IN 30 SECONDS

ChiChi (40) and DB (48) are on their first real date, at a candlelit table high over the Atlanta skyline. It's going beautifully. He's charming, he laughs at her jokes, and he says he has **always wanted a child**, the thing Kel couldn't give her. Then the truth comes out, gently: **work always came first**, and it ended his marriage. He's in therapy and working on it. She asks him straight out: *"Which is more important? Work, or your relationships?"* He answers honestly: **"Work is just as important."** Nobody fights and nobody wins. He thinks the date is going well. His phone buzzes, his eyes flick to it, and she looks down at her bare left hand.

**What it's about:** ChiChi wants love, a partner with time for her, and a child. DB offers one fully, one partly, and maybe not the other. For the first time, she's the one who has to decide what she'll settle for.
