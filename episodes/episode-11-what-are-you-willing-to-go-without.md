# EPISODE 11: "WHAT ARE YOU WILLING TO GO WITHOUT"

**Series:** The Exclusive Series
**Characters:** CHI-CHI, DB, SERVER
**Location:** An upscale restaurant, evening

---

## FADE IN:

### INT. UPSCALE RESTAURANT — NIGHT

Soft amber lighting. White tablecloths. A pianist plays somewhere in the background. Crystal glasses catch the glow of a single candle between them.

**CHI-CHI** (late 30s, poised, polished, effortlessly put together) sits across from **DB** (early 40s, tailored suit, easy smile, the calm confidence of a man used to running rooms). Their entrées are half finished. A bottle of red sits between them.

DB leans back and studies her with genuine curiosity.

**DB**
Okay. I've done most of the talking tonight. Your turn.

**CHI-CHI**
*(smiling, swirling her wine)*
My turn for what?

**DB**
I want to know who Chi-Chi is. Not the résumé version. The real version.

Chi-Chi sets her glass down. She thinks about it for a moment, deciding how honest she wants to be.

**CHI-CHI**
The real version? I've been single for a while.

**DB**
By choice?

**CHI-CHI**
*(a small laugh)*
That's the question, isn't it? I'd like to say yes. I have pretty high standards. I know what I want, and I don't settle.

*(beat)*

But lately I've been wondering if those standards are the reason I'm still sitting in the single pool.

**DB**
And how do you feel about that?

**CHI-CHI**
*(dry)*
Honestly? I refuse to be one of those women with ten cats and no love interest.

DB laughs, a real, unguarded laugh. A couple at the next table glances over. He raises a hand in apology, still grinning.

**DB**
Ten cats. That's specific.

**CHI-CHI**
I've done the math.

**DB**
*(settling, warmer now)*
Well, if it makes you feel any better, I'm in a similar boat.

**CHI-CHI**
Are you?

**DB**
No kids. And I've always wanted one.

Chi-Chi's eyebrows lift, just slightly. That gets her attention.

**DB (CONT'D)**
But work has always gotten in the way of my relationships. I'm building something, and it takes a lot out of me. So at this point I'm just looking for someone who understands that my life is busy.

Chi-Chi nods slowly. On the surface she's composed. Underneath, two things are happening at once.

**CHI-CHI (V.O.)**
He wants a child. *Check.*

*(beat)*

But "my life is busy" is never just a sentence. It's a warning label.

She picks up her fork, turns it over, then sets it back down.

**CHI-CHI**
Can I ask you something?

**DB**
Anything.

**CHI-CHI**
Is that why your marriage ended?

A pause. DB's smile fades. Not defensive, just honest. He looks down at the candle for a moment.

**DB**
Yes.

*(beat)*

She wanted a lot of time. And I just... couldn't give it to her. Not the way she needed. I was always on a call, or on a flight, or "almost done."

**CHI-CHI**
*(softly)*
That's a hard thing to admit.

**DB**
It is. But I've been working on it. I've been going to therapy.

Chi-Chi tilts her head, surprised and a little impressed.

**DB (CONT'D)**
It's really helped. I understand myself better. Why I bury myself in work. What I'm actually running from.

**CHI-CHI**
That says a lot about you.

**DB**
Hopefully something good.

The SERVER approaches, refills their water glasses, and quietly slips away. Chi-Chi waits until they're alone again.

She folds her hands on the table and looks right at him.

**CHI-CHI**
So which one is more important to you? Work, or your relationships?

DB blinks. Then he lets out a short, surprised laugh and leans back.

**DB**
Wow. I don't think anyone's ever been that forward with me.

**CHI-CHI**
*(not backing down, a small smile)*
I'm not twenty-five anymore. I don't have time to wonder.

DB respects that. He takes a breath and gives her a real answer.

**DB**
I think relationships are important. I do.

*(beat)*

But I also feel like work is just as important. I don't think I can pretend otherwise. Not if I'm being honest with you.

The words land. Chi-Chi keeps her expression perfectly pleasant, but something behind her eyes shifts.

**CLOSE ON CHI-CHI.**

The piano keeps playing. The room carries on around her. For her, it has gone very quiet.

**CHI-CHI (V.O.)**
*Just as important.*

*(beat)*

I've spent years wanting it all. The love. The partnership. The family.

*(beat)*

And I never once stopped to ask myself... what if I can't have all three? What if wanting everything means I have to choose?

She lifts her wine glass, takes a slow sip, and puts on a smile for DB.

**CHI-CHI**
*(lightly)*
I appreciate the honesty.

**DB**
*(relieved)*
I'd rather you know now than find out later.

**CHI-CHI**
So would I.

He smiles and reaches for the dessert menu. She watches him and lets her smile fade, just a little.

**CHI-CHI (V.O.)**
So the real question isn't what I want.

*(beat)*

It's what I'm willing to go without.

**HOLD on Chi-Chi's face in the candlelight.**

## CUT TO BLACK.

**END OF EPISODE 11.**

---

### Production Notes

- **Tone:** Romantic and elegant, with an undercurrent of tension. The date is going *well*; the sting is internal, not a fight.
- **DB** should never read as a villain. He's honest, self-aware, and actively working on himself. The tension comes from incompatible priorities, not bad intentions.
- **Chi-Chi's V.O.** carries the emotional arc: interest → worry → realization. Keep her outward reactions controlled and graceful. The audience should feel the gap between what she shows and what she's thinking.
- **Key visual beats:**
  1. DB's big laugh at the "ten cats" line (the warmest moment)
  2. Chi-Chi's eyebrow lift at "I've always wanted one"
  3. DB looking down at the candle when he talks about his ex-wife
  4. The final close-up as the room goes quiet for Chi-Chi
- **Closing line / caption hook:** *"What are you willing to go without?"* Works as an on-screen title card or a social caption to drive comments.
