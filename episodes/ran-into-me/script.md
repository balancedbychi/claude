# EXCLUSIVE with Nia and Chi: "Ran Into Me"

**Format:** scripted short, vertical 9:16
**Runtime:** about 70 seconds, **3 segments** (30 s, 30 s, 12 s). Each segment is one render with cuts inside it. Film one segment at a time.
**When:** a few days after "Miscommunication"
**Cast:** Nia · ChiChi · DB (first appearance)
**Set:** an upscale city sidewalk, sunny afternoon. *(Set element pending your upload.)*

---

## Logline

On a walk, Nia tells ChiChi about Dorian's "year and a half." ChiChi tells her to start dating again, then admits that Kel doesn't want children. Mid-sentence, ChiChi walks straight into a stranger in a very good suit.

---

## Staging (rules 4–6)

- **They walk side by side the whole scene,** ChiChi on the **left of frame**, Nia on the **right**. Nia is visibly shorter (petite and curvy); ChiChi is taller and full-figured.
- **Camera:** a smooth, steady side-on tracking shot moving at their walking pace, eye level, medium-wide and waist-up. It keeps the same distance throughout, with **no zooms or push-ins**. There are a few clean cuts to waist-up walking singles for key lines.
- **No phones,** no props in hand. Nia might carry a small bag on her shoulder, depending on her wardrobe.
- **Pacing:** replies land on each other. The only beats are the ones written below, **half a second each.**

---

## Script

### SEGMENT 1 — "A YEAR AND A HALF" (0:00–0:30)
*EXT. CITY SIDEWALK — AFTERNOON. Nia and ChiChi walk side by side, mid-conversation.*

> **NIA**
> So Dorian just shows up at my door. Flowers and everything.

> **CHICHI**
> Okay…?

> **NIA**
> And he admits he's been seeing someone else. For a year and a half.

> **CHICHI**
> *(stops short a half-step, then keeps walking)*
> A year and a half?

> **NIA**
> Yeah. A year and a half.

> **CHICHI**
> Wow. That's insane. I would hope you'd break up with him.

*ChiChi looks at Nia. Nia looks away. (half-second beat)*

> **CHICHI**
> Nia. What are you doing? He is literally stringing you along.

> **NIA**
> No — I really feel like he actually likes me.

> **CHICHI**
> *(cutting in)*
> Listen. If you want to keep going with him, do whatever you want to do. But can you please at least start dating again?

> **NIA**
> *(groans)*
> I know, I know. I really need to put myself out there. See if I can have my own man.

> **CHICHI**
> *(laughs)*
> Don't we all?

---

### SEGMENT 2 — "THAT'S THAT" (0:30–1:00)
*Still walking.*

> **NIA**
> So what happened with you and Kel?

*ChiChi looks away. (half-second beat)*

> **NIA**
> You know Kel called me, right?

> **CHICHI**
> *(surprised, turning to her)*
> Wait — what?

> **NIA**
> Yeah. Kel called me.

> **CHICHI**
> Yeah, well, Kel also should've told you when he called that he doesn't want to have children.

*Nia stops dead. ChiChi takes one more step, then turns back to her.*

> **NIA**
> He told you he didn't want kids?

> **CHICHI**
> Yep. He doesn't want kids.

*They start walking again.*

> **NIA**
> Okay. Well… I guess that's that, then.

> **CHICHI**
> Yep. That's that.

> **NIA**
> Are you serious? That's it? You're not even gonna fight for your man?

> **CHICHI**
> *(laughs)*
> Well, he would've had to become my man first.

---

### SEGMENT 3 — "RAN INTO ME" (1:00–1:12)
*Still laughing, ChiChi glances at Nia and walks shoulder-first into a MAN standing at the curb. He turns around: DB, tall, in a sharp, expensive suit.*

> **DB**
> Oh — I'm so sorry.
> *(he looks at her properly, and smiles)*
> But I'm so glad you ran into me.

*ChiChi, caught, starts to smile. Behind her, Nia's face says it all: you have GOT to be kidding me.*

*CUT TO BLACK on Nia's look.* **END CARD:** *EXCLUSIVE with Nia and Chi*
