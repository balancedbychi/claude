# EXCLUSIVE with Nia and Chi — "Double Booked"

**Format:** scripted short, vertical 9:16
**Runtime:** ~74 seconds, 5 clips: C1–C2 are continuous takes of 20–22 s, and C3–C5 are 9–12 s
**Cast:** Nia · Dorian · Simone · ChiChi · Kel · Narrator (V.O. only)
**Two storylines, one night:** Nia at Dorian's loft (C1–C2) · ChiChi and Kel at her apartment (C3–C5)

## Logline

Nia shows up at Dorian's to spend the night and finds Simone answering his door. Across town, ChiChi's date with Kel is going beautifully until she asks him the one question that matters.

## Script

### C1 — THE DOOR (0:00–0:20) · one continuous take, no cuts
*Set: `Dorian-Hallway-Night`, ending at the threshold of `Dorian-Loft-Entry-v3`*

*INT. DORIAN'S BUILDING, 10TH-FLOOR HALLWAY — NIGHT. The camera follows Nia down the hallway toward the door of 10B, an overnight bag on her shoulder. She stops, checks her lip gloss in her phone's dark screen and smiles to herself.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> He said come over. He said stay the night.
> He just didn't say it to *only* her.

*She reaches for the handle, but the door swings open first. SIMONE stands in the doorway, barefoot and relaxed, holding a glass of red wine.*

> **SIMONE**
> *(bright, friendly)*
> Oh! Hi! Who are you?

> **NIA**
> *(flat)*
> Who are *you?*

*Nia doesn't wait for an answer. She steps straight past Simone's shoulder into the loft, and the camera follows her in. Simone turns, still smiling and confused.*

> **SIMONE**
> Okay — come in!

---

### C2 — SHE WALKS (0:20–0:42) · one continuous take, no cuts
*Set: `Dorian-Loft-Entry-v3`, looking toward `Dorian-Kitchen-Night`*

*Inside the loft entry. Nia stands just inside with her bag still on her shoulder and Simone behind her at the open door. DORIAN steps out of the kitchen with a dish towel over his shoulder, wiping his hands and calling toward the door.*

> **DORIAN**
> Babe, who was at the —

*He sees Nia and stops short. His eyes go from Nia to Simone and back. He gets it.*

> **DORIAN**
> *(quietly)*
> Nia.

> **SIMONE**
> *(to Dorian, still pleasant)*
> You know her?

*Nia's jaw tightens and her eyes fill, but she doesn't let the tears fall.*

> **NIA**
> *(barely)*
> You told me to come.

*Dorian opens his mouth, but nothing comes out. Nia turns, walks straight past Simone and out the door, and it slams. Simone flinches. The take ends on Dorian and Simone standing apart.*

---

### C3 — ACROSS TOWN (0:42–0:53)
*Set: `ChiChi-Living-Room-Night-v2`*

*INT. CHICHI'S LIVING ROOM — NIGHT. The city lights are on and one warm lamp glows. ChiChi and KEL sit together at the right end of the sectional, each holding a glass of wine. ChiChi is laughing, really laughing.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Meanwhile, ChiChi was having the best night she'd had in a year.

> **KEL**
> — and the pot was *fine.* I watched that pot all night.

*ChiChi laughs and settles back, studying him. She decides to ask.*

> **CHICHI**
> Kel. Do you want children?

---

### C4 — THE QUESTION (0:53–1:05)
*Set: `ChiChi-Living-Room-Night-v2`*

*Kel smiles gently, as if she's joking.*

> **KEL**
> You do know I'm fifty-three, right?

> **CHICHI**
> *(evenly)*
> That wasn't my question. Do you want children?

*Kel sets his glass down and leans in, sincere and kind.*

> **KEL**
> Look, I really like where this is going. I do.
> But don't you think we're a little too old for that?

---

### C5 — TAG (1:05–1:14)
*Set: `ChiChi-Living-Room-Night-v2`*

*Close on ChiChi. She looks at him with no anger, only clarity.*

> **CHICHI**
> *(quietly)*
> Wow. I don't know if this is gonna work.

*Kel's smile fades.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> One woman found out there was someone else.
> The other found out there wasn't going to be.

*CUT TO BLACK.*

**END CARD:** *EXCLUSIVE with Nia and Chi*
