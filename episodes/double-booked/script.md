# EXCLUSIVE with Nia and Chi — "Double Booked"

**Format:** scripted short, vertical 9:16
**Runtime:** ~90 seconds, 7 clips (C1–C7) of ~12–13 s each
**Cast:** Nia · Dorian · Simone · ChiChi · Kel · Narrator (V.O. only)
**Two storylines, one night:** Nia at Dorian's loft (C1–C4) · ChiChi and Kel at her apartment (C5–C7)

---

## Logline

Nia shows up at Dorian's to spend the night and finds Simone answering his door. Across town, ChiChi's date with Kel is going beautifully until she asks him the one question that matters.

## Script

### C1 — THE DOOR (0:00–0:13)
*Set: `Dorian-Hallway-Night`*

*INT. DORIAN'S BUILDING, 10TH-FLOOR HALLWAY — NIGHT. Nia walks to the door of 10B with an overnight bag on her shoulder. She stops, checks her lip gloss in her phone camera, and smiles to herself.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> He said come over. He said stay the night.
> *(beat)*
> He just didn't say it to *only* her.

*Nia reaches for the handle, but the door swings open before she touches it.*

*SIMONE stands in the doorway, barefoot and glowing, holding a glass of wine.*

---

### C2 — WHO ARE YOU? (0:13–0:26)
*Set: `Dorian-Hallway-Night` → `Dorian-Loft-Entry-v3`*

*Two-shot across the threshold.*

> **SIMONE**
> *(bright, friendly)*
> Oh! Hi! …Who are you?

> **NIA**
> *(flat)*
> Who are *you?*

*Simone opens her mouth to answer, but Nia is already moving and steps right past her shoulder into the loft. Simone turns and watches her go, still smiling and confused.*

> **SIMONE**
> Okay — come in!

---

### C3 — THE KITCHEN (0:26–0:39)
*Set: `Dorian-Loft-Entry-v3` → `Dorian-Kitchen-Night`*

*DORIAN steps out of the kitchen with a dish towel over his shoulder, wiping his hands and calling back toward the door.*

> **DORIAN**
> Babe, who was at the —

*He sees Nia and stops dead. The towel stops moving in his hands.*

*He looks at Nia, then at Simone, then back at Nia. We watch him work it out: two texts, and the same night.*

> **DORIAN**
> *(quietly)*
> …Nia.

> **SIMONE**
> *(to Dorian, still pleasant)*
> You know her?

---

### C4 — SHE WALKS (0:39–0:52)
*Set: `Dorian-Loft-Entry-v3`*

*Close on Nia as she looks at Dorian. Her jaw is tight and her eyes fill. She won't let it fall, not here.*

> **NIA**
> *(barely)*
> You told me to come.

*Dorian opens his mouth, but nothing comes out. He doesn't have a defence, and he knows it.*

*Nia turns and walks straight past Simone and out the door. Simone flinches as it slams.*

*Hold on Dorian and Simone, who stand in silence.*

---

### C5 — ACROSS TOWN (0:52–1:05)
*Set: `ChiChi-Living-Room-Night-v2`*

*INT. CHICHI'S LIVING ROOM — NIGHT. The city lights are on and one warm lamp glows. ChiChi and KEL sit together at the right end of the sectional, each holding a glass of wine. ChiChi is laughing, really laughing.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Meanwhile, ChiChi was having the best night she'd had in a year.

> **KEL**
> — and the pot was *fine.* I watched that pot all night.

*ChiChi laughs and settles back, studying him. She decides to ask.*

> **CHICHI**
> Kel. Do you want children?

---

### C6 — THE QUESTION (1:05–1:18)
*Set: `ChiChi-Living-Room-Night-v2`*

*Kel pauses, then smiles gently, as if she's joking.*

> **KEL**
> You do know I'm fifty-three, right?

> **CHICHI**
> *(evenly)*
> That wasn't my question.
> *(beat)*
> Do you want children?

*Kel sets his glass down and leans in, sincere and kind.*

> **KEL**
> Look… I really like where this is going. I do.
> But don't you think we're a little too old for that?

---

### C7 — TAG (1:18–1:30)
*Set: `ChiChi-Living-Room-Night-v2`*

*Close on ChiChi. She takes a long look at him with no anger, only clarity.*

> **CHICHI**
> *(quietly)*
> Wow.
> *(beat)*
> I don't know if this is gonna work.

*Kel's smile fades.*

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> One woman found out there was someone else.
> The other found out there wasn't going to be.

*CUT TO BLACK.*

**END CARD:** *EXCLUSIVE with Nia and Chi*

## Continuity and production notes

These come from the locked rules in the Higgsfield element notes.

- **Sets:** `Dorian-Hallway-Night` is locked as supplied, with no relight. `Dorian-Loft-Entry-v3` overrides `-Entry-v2` and `-Entry-Night`, which are retired. `Dorian-Kitchen-Night`. `ChiChi-Living-Room-Night-v2` has **no floor lamp**, the TV stays off, and the room is lit by one table lamp.
- **Dorian:** use `Dorian` for identity. **He is not a villain.** He's caught, embarrassed and out of words, but never smirking or cold. The audience should see a man who simply didn't think it through.
- **Simone:** `Simone-Face-v3` supplies her face and hair only, as a copper-auburn bob. She stays **cheerful and friendly** the whole time, with no sneer and no smugness. She's as blindsided as Nia, just slower to notice. Her wardrobe has **no coat**, because she has been there a while.
- **Kel:** use `Kel-The-Cousin`. He's 53, warm and sincere, and **genuinely kind**. His answer is honest, not dismissive. His joke about the pot in C5 calls back to "The Caterer".
- **ChiChi:** use `ChiChi-the-Influencer` for identity and `ChiChi-Face-Photoreal` for skin. Her hair is honey-blonde, pending your answer on the hair question. **No rings, no bracelets.** She wants a child and has no partner, which carries over from "Available".
- **Nia:** use `Nia` for identity. **No rings, no bracelets.** She carries the overnight bag in C1, and it should stay on her shoulder through C4.
- **Wine:** Simone holds one glass in C1–C4. ChiChi and Kel have exactly two glasses in C5–C7. Levels only ever go down.
- **Phone:** Nia uses her phone as a mirror in C1. **Never show readable text on the screen.**
- **Wardrobe:** this is a new episode, so no one has a locked look yet. Every character needs wardrobe elements before generation. Dorian's black set and Kel's polo are locked to "The Caterer" only.

**Spoken word count:** about 220 words, which fits 90 seconds with room for the silences. C4 and C7 depend on the pauses, so don't rush them.
