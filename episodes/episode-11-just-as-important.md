# EXCLUSIVE · Episode 11 · "Just As Important"

**Clips:** 6 · **Runtime:** about 2 min 10 s · **Format:** two-hander, one table · **Time:** night
**Cast:** ChiChi, DB, one server (no lines)
**Picks up from:** "Tables Turned." DB called from his law office and asked her to dinner, and he would explain the lawyer story "over dinner." CHICHI: "Okay. I'll see you tonight." This is that dinner.

---

## Where we are

- **ChiChi (40)** has been single a while. In "Ran Into Me" she told Nia that Kel "doesn't want to have children," and she let him go: *"He would've had to become my man first."* She wants a partner **and** a family. Back in "Eleven Days" we saw her alone in a beautiful, quiet apartment with nowhere else to be.
- **DB (48)** literally ran into her on a park sidewalk (*"But I'm so glad you ran into me"*) and called her a few days later from his corner office. He's a Dominican lawyer: divorced, extremely busy, kind, attentive, self-assured, never smug.
- Tonight is their first real date.

## Episode outline

| Beat | Clip | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 01 | She calls in the promise: the lawyer story. He turns it back on her: *"Who is Chi?"* |
| Setup | 02 | High standards, single a while, ten cats. He laughs for real. |
| Complication | 03–04 | He wants a child (*check*). Work has always come first (*worry*). It's why his marriage ended. He's in therapy. |
| Turn | 05 | *"Work, or your relationships?"* / *"Just as important."* |
| Button | 06 | The date goes on happily for him. For her, the room goes quiet. |

- **Theme question:** What do you accept when you don't believe you deserve more? This episode flips it: what do you give up when you *do*? Chi has always wanted all three: love, a partner with time for her, and a child. DB offers one cleanly, one partly, and maybe not the third.
- **Character beat:** ChiChi, the one who always tells Nia the truth, hears a truth she didn't ask for. For the first time she's the one who has to decide what she'll settle for.
- **Callbacks:** the lawyer story he owed her over dinner ("Tables Turned"). *"I'm not twenty-five anymore"* lands on Nia's twenty-five-year-old ("Tables Turned"/"Rented"). Kel and the no-kids line ("Ran Into Me"). Her held-then-adjusted smile mirrors Nia's in "The Caterer," Clip 4. Her bare left hand.
- **Hand-off:** ChiChi leaves dinner with a question, not an answer. Meanwhile Nia was just told by Tay, *"Maybe I ain't tryna be rented."* Both women are now facing a choice.

## The script

### Clip 01 · The Lawyer Story · ~18 s
**INT. FINE-DINING RESTAURANT · CORNER TABLE · NIGHT**

A hushed, expensive room: white tablecloths, low amber light, one candle between them. ChiChi and DB at a two-top, entrées half finished, a glass of red each. DB's phone lies **face-down** beside his plate. He never touches it in this clip.

**CHICHI:** "You owe me the lawyer story. You said over dinner."
**DB:** "I did say that."
**CHICHI:** "So how long have you been a lawyer?"
**DB:** "Long enough that I'd rather hear about you."
**CHICHI:** "Mm. Smooth."
**DB:** *(a small smile)* "Honest. I've done all the talking. Who is Chi?"

> **Direction:** He's deflecting work talk, and the episode is going to be about work, so let that irony sit unnoticed. "Mm." is ChiChi's established sound from Episode 1. Keep it identical.

---

### Clip 02 · Ten Cats · ~22 s
**SAME · CONTINUOUS**

ChiChi sets her glass down and takes one beat to decide how honest to be.

**CHICHI:** "Honestly? I've been single for a while."
**DB:** "By choice?"
**CHICHI:** "I'd like to say yes. My standards are high."
**CHICHI:** "Lately I wonder if they're why I'm still single."
**CHICHI:** *(dry)* "And I refuse to be the woman with ten cats."

DB laughs, a real one from the chest, head tipping back. Nothing nervous about it.

**DB:** "Ten? That is very specific."
**CHICHI:** "I've done the math."

> **Direction:** DB's laugh is the most important half-second of the clip, just like Dorian's in "The Caterer." It has to make him likeable. Chi isn't fishing for reassurance. She's dry about it, and that's why he laughs.

---

### Clip 03 · Always Wanted One · ~22 s · ends in silence
**SAME · CONTINUOUS**

DB settles. Warmer now, leaning in slightly on his forearms.

**DB:** "If it helps, I'm in a similar boat."
**DB:** "No children. And I have always wanted one."

**REACTION, CHICHI:** her brows lift a fraction and she goes still. That one landed.

**DB:** "But work always got in the way of my relationships."
**DB:** "I want someone who understands my life is busy."

Hold on ChiChi, about two seconds, no dialogue. Her pleasant smile holds, then **adjusts by about five percent.** Her eyes flick once to his face-down phone and back.

> **Direction:** Two things land in four lines: *he wants a child* (the box Kel couldn't check) and *"my life is busy"* (a warning label). Play both in her face, not in a line. No smirk, no eye-roll. The audience does the work.

---

### Clip 04 · Why It Ended · ~26 s
**SAME · CONTINUOUS**

**CHICHI:** "Can I ask you something?"
**DB:** "Anything."
**CHICHI:** "Is that why your marriage ended?"

DB's smile fades. He's not defensive, just honest. He looks down at the candle.

**DB:** "Yes."
**DB:** "She wanted a lot of time. I couldn't give it."
**DB:** *(looking back up at her)* "I've been working on that. I'm in therapy."
**CHICHI:** *(surprised, a little impressed)* "Therapy."
**DB:** "It's helped. More than I expected."

> **Direction:** This is DB at his most attractive: self-aware, no excuses, actually doing the work. If he reads as wounded or as performing growth, the turn in Clip 05 becomes a gotcha. It has to be a good man telling the truth.

---

### Clip 05 · Just As Important · ~30 s
**SAME · CONTINUOUS**

The SERVER refills both water glasses and slips away without a word. ChiChi waits until they're alone, folds her hands on the tablecloth, and looks right at him.

**CHICHI:** "Which is more important? Work, or your relationships?"

DB blinks, lets out a short surprised laugh, and leans back.

**DB:** "Nobody has ever been that forward with me."
**CHICHI:** *(not backing down, small smile)* "I'm not twenty-five anymore."
**DB:** *(he respects that; a breath)* "Relationships are important. I believe that."
**DB:** "But work is just as important. I won't pretend otherwise."

**REACTION CHAIN, CHICHI:**
1. *Trigger:* "just as important."
2. *Instant:* the smile holds, but her eyes stop moving.
3. *Processing:* she glances down at the candle, the same place he looked when he talked about his ex-wife.
4. *Response:* she looks back up, gracious:

**CHICHI:** *(lightly)* "I appreciate the honesty."
**DB:** *(relieved)* "I'd rather you know now."
**CHICHI:** "So would I."

> **Direction:** Nobody wins this exchange. He gave her the most honest answer available and she asked for it. The sting is internal. On screen this is a lovely date between two grown, honest people.

---

### Clip 06 · Three Things · ~12 s · no dialogue
**SAME · CONTINUOUS**

DB picks up the dessert menu, pleased; for him the date is going well. His face-down phone **buzzes once** against the tablecloth. He doesn't pick it up, but his eyes flick to it for half a second.

ChiChi sees it. She watches him across the candle and lets her smile fade, just a little. Then she looks down at her own **bare left hand** resting on the white tablecloth and holds there.

Room sound dips. Only the low piano is left. Hold on ChiChi in the candlelight.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

> **Direction:** This is the episode's version of "Eleven Days" Shot 5: the turn plays in silence. Nobody says the theme out loud. She's realizing that wanting three things may mean choosing which one to go without, and the audience reads it in her face, his phone, and her hand. Don't let the model add a line.
