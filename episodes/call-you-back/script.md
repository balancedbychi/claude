## THE STORY IN 30 SECONDS

It's late on the Saturday DB cleared. ChiChi, who refused to be penciled in, is on her sofa holding his phone, because he handed it to her so he couldn't check it. It buzzes: his office. He doesn't move. *"It is important. It's also Saturday."* She tells him she's out of reasons for today to go wrong. Then her own phone lights up: Nia.

Across town, in the back of a car, Nia is still in the green dress. She says the launch was *"a nice building,"* says it's nothing, and asks how the cleared Saturday is going. ChiChi hears that it isn't nothing, and takes the way out: *"Can I call you back?"* Nia, dry: *"Course. Pencil me in."* DB asks if everything's okay. ChiChi: *"She said it was nothing."* He asks if she believes it. *"She told me to go."*

**What it's about:** ChiChi finally gets what she asked for in Episode 12, a man who gives her his time, and the first thing she does with it is pencil her best friend in. Theme check: ChiChi chooses what she wants, and it costs Nia the one call she needed.

**How it ties to the other episodes:**
- Episode 13 promised two Saturdays (*"Does Saturday hold for both women?"*). Episode 14 showed Nia's. This is ChiChi's.
- The audience knows what Nia isn't saying, because Episode 14 just showed it. Her *"It was a nice building"* is Episode 14's title turned into a wound.
- It sets up Episode 16: Dorian's answer to *"You still up?"*, with Nia alone because her friend asked to call her back.
