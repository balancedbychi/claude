## THE STORY IN 30 SECONDS

DB cleared the whole Saturday and takes ChiChi to the Atlanta Art Museum. In front of a huge collage picture of two figures at work, she says what she sees: *"Little pieces up close. You have to give it time."* His phone buzzes. *"Sorry. One second."* At the next picture she starts to tell him something true, *"I used to come to places like this by myself on Sundays. I'd always imagine—"*, and he says *"Hold on"* without looking up. On the bench she asks if she can say something that isn't about the art, and he has to take a call and walks away. Alone, she hears his side of it: *"No, it's fine. I'm not doing anything."*

He comes back, pockets the phone, warm and a little out of breath: *"Sorry. That's done. Where were we?"* She turns to him, level: *"Not continuing this."* He's taken aback, leans back, and the episode stops.

**What it's about:** ChiChi asked for time (Episode 12: she won't be penciled in). DB said work is just as important (Episode 11). The Saturday he cleared is the proof, and nobody makes a speech: the phone says it. Theme check: ChiChi chooses what she wants over what she'd settle for, the opposite of Nia's night in Episode 14.

**How it ties to the other episodes:**
- Episode 13 promised *"completely cleared."* This is that promise breaking, one buzz at a time.
- Each of ChiChi's answers gets cooler: *"Go ahead," "Sure," "Of course,"* then silence, then *"Not continuing this."*
- Her first thought, *"You have to give it time,"* comes back in his mouth right after he takes some of hers.
- It plays earlier than Episode 14's night, so the audience sees Nia's Saturday night first and ChiChi's Saturday afternoon second.
- Open on purpose: she never finishes *"I'd always imagine—"*.

**No unnecessary pauses** (your rule from Episode 11): every line starts within about 0.2 s of the one before, and reactions play under the dialogue. Every clip ends on a spoken line, except Clip 05, which ends on his reaction. **Clip lengths match the speaking time** (Episode 12 lesson). **One object moves at a time** (Episode 12 lesson: the phantom coffee pot).

## CLIP 01 · "One Second" · 13 s · 91 credits

**Job:** a good start. They're together, she says something true about the picture, and the first buzz arrives.

His phone `[DB-Phone]` is in the hand away from her, down at his side. One visitor `[Visitors]` looks at the portraits, far back by the orange wall.*

## CLIP 03 · "I Have to Take This" · 13 s · 91 credits

**Job:** the real attempt, and the biggest interruption. He leaves the conversation.

### Script

> **CHICHI** Come sit. Just for a minute.
> **DB** *(sitting on the right cushion, the phone resting on his knee)* Okay. I'm here.
> **CHICHI** *(turning to him, sincere)* Can I say something that isn't about the art?
> *(His phone buzzes and keeps buzzing: a call.)*
> **DB** *(looking at it, already rising)* Hold on. I have to take this.
> **CHICHI** *(level, gracious)* Of course.
> **DB** *(phone to his ear, walking away, hushed)* Yeah. Go ahead.

## CARRY-FORWARD RULES (every clip)

1. **No rings on either of them.** *NO RINGS on any finger of either hand; the fourth finger of the left hand is bare skin; no wedding band, no engagement ring* (rule 3a). ChiChi wears no watch and no bracelets. DB wears his steel bracelet watch (dark dial) on his left wrist.
2. **ChiChi's hair is honey-blonde**, deep side part on the left, American accent. **DB is Dominican**, tall and clearly taller than ChiChi.
3. **Voices:** ChiChi from her saved voice element, DB from his written description, word for word. **Only these lines are spoken.** DB's lines into the phone are hushed, the caller is never heard, and ChiChi is silent in Clip 04.
4. **Pacing:** no silent beats, every clip but 05 ends on a line, and nobody speaks after the last line.
5. **Light:** soft, even gallery light from the track spots. Never night, no hard sun beams.
6. **The art is exactly what the plate shows:** the two-panel picture, the small field picture, the seven portrait prints, the white platform with its stack of boxes, the two benches. Nothing added, moved or swapped. **No readable text anywhere except the printing on the boxes**; the wall labels stay blank. I never name an artist.
7. **The phone:** only DB's, a plain black smartphone. It is in his hand in every clip until he pockets it in Clip 05. No screen ever faces the camera, no text or message bubbles are visible, and the caller is never heard.
8. **Visitors:** one or two, far back, muted clothing (**no orange, no rust, no red**), deep soft focus, looking at the portraits, mouths closed. They never cross in front of the leads, never come within about ten feet of them, and never stand near DB's call spot.
9. **Clothes:** nothing orange or rust on either lead, so neither disappears into the orange wall. Any tan belt or loafers are a muted caramel brown, never orange.
10. **Movement:** ordinary walking pace, feet on the floor. In Clip 02 they walk side by side, the same direction, the same pace. In Clip 03 DB leaves up the room, as scripted. No zooms or push-ins, and no framing tighter than waist-up.
11. **Headcount:** exactly two main people (one ChiChi, one DB), never two of anyone. Reflections in the polished floor are soft colour only. Visitors are background.
12. **Last clip:** nothing is said after "Not continuing this." DB's mouth stays closed, and the clip ends as the lean settles.

- **Nia:** knows nothing of this. Episode 14's night comes later the same day.
