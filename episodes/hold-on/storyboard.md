## Where the stills differ from the prompts (read these before judging)

- **Faces are close, not identical, from frame to frame.** Each still is generated alone. The video uses the elements, so judge the staging here, not the faces.
- **Phone and watch hands:** in 1.2, 2.2 and 2.5 the phone is in DB's nearer hand, which also wears the watch. The video prompts keep the phone in his **right** hand and the watch on his **left** wrist.
- **4.1:** DB is at the right end of the white wall, near the small picture, a little short of the true back corner.
- **5.3:** DB stands head to toe and looks surprised but is not visibly leaning; the video prompt asks for the lean away.
- **The art:** the first pass sometimes painted a different picture or a skylight. The kept frames show the two-panel collage, the small field picture, the orange wall and the platform with its boxes, as in your set.
- **The visitor** is left out of the stills, to keep them clean. The video prompts add one silent visitor far back.
