# 50 Social Media Content Topics

Topics creators use again and again to promote content, grouped by type. The ones marked
**(Trending)** are showing up in trend roundups for September 2026 (sources at the bottom).

## Routines and daily life
1. Morning routine (slow mornings, 5am club, "that girl")
2. Night routine / wind-down
3. GRWM (get ready with me) for an event, work, or a date
4. Day in the life (job, city, or life stage)
5. Sunday reset / weekly reset
6. **(Trending)** "Life lately" photo-dump edits with text-overlay storytelling
7. **(Trending)** Romanticizing ordinary life and mundane habits
8. **(Trending)** Cozy girl autumn: warm drinks, candles, layered outfits, fall walks
9. **(Trending)** "A new season has begun" fall welcome montage
10. What I eat in a day

## Wellness and self-care
11. Small habits that make a massive difference
12. **(Trending)** "10/10 habits for [goal]" list videos
13. Self-care / skincare routine
14. Mental health check-ins and anxiety tips
15. Workout or pilates routine
16. Healthy recipes and meal prep
17. Tea, matcha, or wellness drink rituals
18. Journaling prompts and reflections
19. Sleep hygiene / "sleepy girl" routines
20. **(Trending)** Reflective storytelling, e.g. "what healing actually looked like for me"

## Education and value
21. **(Trending)** "If you're spending hours doing X, try this instead"
22. **(Trending)** "Advice I'd give every new [role/beginner]"
23. Myths vs. facts in your niche
24. Beginner's guide / "how to start [thing]"
25. Things I wish I knew sooner
26. Common mistakes to avoid
27. Tool, app, or product recommendations
28. Step-by-step tutorials
29. Q&A: answering follower questions
30. Behind the scenes of your business or process

## Story and personal brand
31. My story / how I got started
32. Before and after / transformation
33. Small wins celebration
34. Lessons learned from a failure
35. Unpopular opinion in your niche
36. Storytime (personal, funny, or dramatic)
37. POV videos ("POV: you finally...")
38. A letter to my younger self

## Entertainment and humor
39. **(Trending)** Expectation vs. reality split-screen
40. **(Trending)** Very specific, relatable observations turned into humor
41. Trending audio lip-syncs or skits
42. "Things that just make sense" montages
43. ASMR (unboxing, pouring, organizing, cooking)
44. Duets and stitches reacting to other creators

## Promotion and community
45. Product in use / honest review
46. Unboxing and first impressions
47. Favorites of the month
48. Shop my home, closet, or shelf (shoppable)
49. Giveaways and collaborations
50. Challenges (7-day, 30-day, or a trending challenge)

---

**Tip:** You can combine topics. For example, "cozy girl autumn" + night routine + a product
in use gives you a seasonal, shoppable video.
