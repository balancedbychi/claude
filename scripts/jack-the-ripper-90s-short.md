# 90-Second Short — "Fourpence" (Jack the Ripper, 1888)

Vertical companion to `jack-the-ripper-time-travel-vlog.md`. Same host, same
world, one idea.

---

## SPEC

| | |
|---|---|
| **Runtime** | 0:90 |
| **Aspect** | 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920 |
| **VO** | ~225 words @ ~155 wpm, first person, present tense |
| **Shots** | 12 × ~7–8s — sized for one generation each |
| **Captions** | Burned in, center-lower, heavy white sans with hard black stroke |
| **Title** | `Fourpence was the difference between a bed and the street #history` |
| **Alt title** | `I time travelled to Whitechapel in 1888 #history` |

**Why this idea and not the murders:** it's the one fact in the whole story a
viewer hasn't heard, it recontextualizes everything they *have* heard, and it
carries no gore. Same safety rules as the long video — no attack shown,
described, or implied on screen; the victims are people, not a body count.

**Loop design:** the last line ("It was always fourpence") hands straight back
to the first line ("comes down to four pennies"). On replay it reads as one
continuous sentence. Shorts are scored on loops — build for it.

**Character reference:** identical sheet to the long-form video — same face,
same forearm tattoos, dark wool dress, cream shawl, hair pinned. Load it as the
character reference on every shot. Grade: night = yellow-green sodium fog, deep
crushed shadow, lamp bloom; day = desaturated grey-brown haze, soot on
everything. Never clean, never pretty.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**The three-second test.** Shot 01 has to work with sound off and no context.
The caption `WHITECHAPEL, 1888` plus a frightened face plus fog does the whole
job. If the first generation comes back with her looking *curious* rather than
*unnerved*, regenerate — curiosity doesn't hold a thumb.

**Caption timing.** Burn each caption in on the shot's first frame, not
progressively — Shorts viewers read faster than the VO speaks, and a caption
that arrives late reads as a mistake.

**Audio.** Low sustained drone under 01–07. Cut to near-silence on shot 08. Keep
it silent through 09. Bring the drone back under 11 and let it resolve on 12.
No music sting on the Nichols line — the silence is the sting.

**Pacing.** Shots 01–07 average 6.5s and should feel brisk. Shots 08–12 average
8s and should feel slower. That deceleration is the entire emotional structure;
don't even it out in the edit.

**What to cut if it runs long.** Shot 03 (the 80,000 figure) first — it's
context, not spine. Then shot 10. Never cut 06, 07, or 08; those three are the
argument.

**Accuracy.** The Thrawl Street turn-away and the "jolly bonnet" line are both
documented. 233 lodging houses in Whitechapel is the contemporary police figure.
Fourpence for a single bed is standard and widely attested. The claim that all
five deaths are the work of one person is disputed by some historians — this
Short says "five women were killed here," which is true and sidesteps it.
