# EXCLUSIVE — the new order
## Episode 1: "White"

**Status: PROPOSED — nothing locked, nothing spent.** Written 15 Sep 2026 to the
user's brief for a juicier season. Read `exclusive/new-order-README.md` first; it
carries the season's one new fact, and this episode is unreadable without it.

**Runtime:** 1:34 · 6 clips · 94 seconds
**Format:** One phone call, then one apartment, one continuous night.
**Logline:** Dorian uses the word "exclusive." Nia wears white to celebrate. Ninety
seconds later another woman is barefoot in his kitchen, and the last two words of
the episode are "Hey, Chi."

---

## THE SHAPE

**The happy part is real and it has to be played completely straight.** Clips 1 and
2 are the warmest, most genuinely lovely material the series has ever had — Chi
laughing, Dorian in a good mood, Nia winning. If the audience does not want this
for her, nothing that follows costs anything. **Do not seed a single note of dread
into Clip 1 or 2.** The only dread is the half-second after Chi hangs up, and it is
in her face, not in the scene.

Then it goes in three steps, each one ratcheting: someone knows something (C3),
someone arrives who belongs here (C4), someone says the dates out loud (C5), and
then the wine (C6).

**The episode's real subject is not the wine.** It is that Nia spends the entire
back half looking at the wrong woman.

---

## WHAT IT INHERITS AND WHAT IT PLANTS

**Inherits:** Chi's midnight exit and her "Have you." · Dorian's "my friend Nia" ·
the counting · Chi's stillness as her whole vocabulary · the fact that Chi's drink
stopping halfway is the loudest thing she can do.

**Plants:**
- **"Bold."** (C1) — the first lie of the season, one syllable, and the audience
  will not catch it until N4.
- **"That's not the same sentence."** (C3) — Dorian starts the season's motif and
  he is the only one who never uses it against anyone.
- **Simone's shoes** (C4) — she knows where they go.
- **"We've met." / "Here."** (C6) — Chi lies on camera, in front of a witness, and
  it is technically true. **This is the line N4 detonates.**

---

## THE SCRIPT

Six clips, 94 seconds, one continuous evening. Panavision Millennium XL2, Primo
lenses, eye-level throughout, night key and warm practicals from Clip 2 on. Cuts
land on the first syllable of the incoming line. **Three holds are allowed and no
more: the end of C1, the beat before the pour in C6, and the last four seconds.**

### CLIP 01 — "Bold" · 18s · 40mm · f/2.0 · intercut
**Int. Nia's bedroom — sunset / Int. Chi's living room — sunset.**
`Nia-Bedroom-Sunset` + `ChiChi-Living-Room`. The Episode-1 format, deliberately:
two apartments, two phones visible in frame, cut on the first syllable.

Nia at the mirror, dressed, radiant. Chi folded into the corner of her sofa, one
glass of red, one candle. Same as the first night of the series. Nothing has moved.

> **NIA:** He said it. He said the word.
> **CHI** *(phone)*: What word?
> **NIA:** Exclusive. Out loud. With his mouth.
> **CHI** *(phone, and she laughs — real, from the chest)*: Okay.
> **NIA:** Say it like you mean it.
> **CHI** *(phone)*: I mean it. I'm happy for you.
> *(beat)*
> **CHI** *(phone)*: What are you wearing?
> **NIA:** White.
> **CHI** *(phone, one beat too long)*: ...Bold.

Nia hangs up mid-shriek. **Stay with Chi.** She lowers the phone. She does not put
it down — she holds it, face down, in her lap. The smile goes. She looks at the
candle. Hold three full seconds.

*Chi's laugh in this clip is the most important half-second in the episode and it
must be genuinely warm — she is not faking it, she IS happy for her, and that is
exactly why the rest is a tragedy. The performance note is: **everything Chi says
here is true until "Bold."** Then: never a smirk, never a wince, never a look to
camera. The smile does not collapse, it stops being maintained. She is doing
arithmetic.*

*"Bold." is the whole episode title said as a one-word lie. Flat, dry, affectionate
on the surface. If it lands as a warning the clip is broken.*

### CLIP 02 — "A Dare" · 14s · 50mm · f/2.0 · two cuts
**Int. Dorian's loft, window end — night.** `Dorian-Loft-Window-Night`. Early,
before anyone else is here. Warm practicals, the city behind the glass.

Nia just arrived, coat still over her arm. Dorian across the room, whiskey tumbler,
unhurried. He is 6'2" and she is not — the difference holds in every frame.

> **DORIAN:** Turn around.
> **NIA:** Why?
> **DORIAN:** Because I asked.
> *(she turns — slow, enjoying it, one full rotation)*
> **DORIAN:** White.
> **NIA:** Is that a problem?
> **DORIAN:** It's a statement.
> **NIA:** It's a dress.
> **DORIAN:** No.
> *(he crosses to her and stops just short — close enough that she has to look up)*
> **DORIAN:** That's a dare.

He does not touch her. The clip ends there.

*The sexual tension is entirely in the distance he closes and the contact he does
not make. **No kiss, no hand on the waist, no touch of any kind** — the model will
offer one and it must be negated. He stops about a foot away and they both stay
there. That is hotter than anything it could render, and it is also the character:
Dorian gets very close to things and does not commit to them.*

*He is warm, funny, plainly delighted by her. **He is not being sinister and there
is no double meaning in "dare."** He means it as a compliment. He is telling the
truth. He is always telling the truth; he just never volunteers any of it.*

### CLIP 03 — "Not the Same Sentence" · 14s · 40mm · f/2.0 · two cuts
**Int. Dorian's loft, staircase end — night.** `Dorian-Loft-Night`. Chi has just
arrived. Nia is off-frame somewhere with someone's coat. These two are alone for
the first time on screen and the temperature drops about eight degrees.

> **DORIAN:** Chi.
> **CHI:** Dorian.
> *(nothing. four full seconds. neither looks away and neither moves.)*
> **DORIAN:** She looks happy.
> **CHI:** She is happy.
> **DORIAN:** That's not the same sentence.
> **CHI:** No. It isn't.

He offers her a drink. She takes it. **Their hands do not touch, and both of them
make sure of it** — she takes the glass by the base, he lets go early. Cut.

*⚠️ **THIS CLIP IS THE SEASON AND IT IS FOUR SECONDS OF SILENCE.** The silence must
be declared in the prompt or it gets filled: "this clip is the exception, the pause
between 'Dorian.' and 'She looks happy.' is the point, do not fill it, no ambience
swell, no music, no third voice."*

*He says **"Chi"**, not "Chi Chi", not "you must be Chi." One syllable, no
introduction, no greeting. **That single word is the entire retcon delivered
without a line of exposition** and it is why the prologue's "You must be Chi. I've
heard a lot." was a performance. Nobody explains this. Ever.*

*Neither of them is hostile. That is the trap and the note. They are two people who
know each other extremely well being careful in a room, and the care is the tell.
**No glare, no tension in the jaw, no loaded look.** Play it almost friendly. The
four seconds do all of it.*

*The glass handoff is blocking, not symbolism — but pin it, because the model's
default is fingers brushing and that is a different scene.*

### CLIP 04 — "Shoes" · 13s · 40mm · f/2.2 · two cuts
**Int. hallway outside 10B → the loft — night.** `Dorian-Hallway-Night` into the
loft. The door hardware is pinned: **hinges LEFT, handle RIGHT, opens inward**, and
this is shot from the INSIDE, so **every part of that mirrors** — see the warning
in `ep-the-caterer.md`, which cost three takes. Write it from the side you are
shooting.

The door opens. **Simone comes in without knocking and without being let in.**

She is already talking. She sets her bag down in a particular spot. She steps out
of her heels and places them, neatly, side by side, against the wall — **the same
spot she has put them a hundred times.** Barefoot in about four seconds.

> **SIMONE** *(to the room, bright, cheerful)*: It's freezing out.
> **NIA:** I'm sorry — who—
> **SIMONE** *(already barefoot, already moving past her)*: Simone. You must be the
> new one.
> **NIA:** The new what?
> **SIMONE** *(a small delighted laugh, genuinely amused, not unkind)*: He didn't.

*The shoes are the whole clip and they are worth more than the dialogue. **She does
not look down when she takes them off and she does not look at the wall when she
sets them there.** Muscle memory. The audience understands the situation before a
single fact is stated, which is CLAUDE.md §1's "specific beats universal" doing its
job.*

*"You must be the new one" is a deliberate echo of Dorian's "You must be Chi" and
**nobody in the room notices it.***

*⚠️ **Simone's register, which is the easiest thing in this season to get wrong.**
She is cheerful. Bright, warm, normal volume, unhurried, having a perfectly nice
time. **No sneer, no smirk, no drawl, no raised eyebrow, no hair flip, no
head-tilt, no hand on hip, no sashay.** She is not a villain arriving; she is a
woman who lives here arriving. Every one of those defaults will be offered by the
model and every one of them makes her small.*

*"He didn't." is said the way you would say "oh, bless him" about a man who forgot
to mention something at work.*

### CLIP 05 — "Since When" · 17s · 50mm → 75mm · f/2.0 · three cuts
**Int. Dorian's loft, window end — night.** The room has noticed. Guests kept to
the frame edges, deep soft focus, **never more than six**, talking to each other in
an indistinct murmur with not one intelligible word. Dorian is not in this clip and
that is deliberate — **he is being discussed in the third person twelve feet from
where he is standing.**

> **NIA:** I think you should leave.
> **SIMONE:** I think you should ask him how long.
> **NIA:** We're exclusive.
> **SIMONE** *(pleasantly, as though correcting a booking)*: Since when?
> **NIA:** Tuesday.
> *(Simone turns her wrist and actually checks her watch. She is not being
> theatrical. She is genuinely working it out.)*
> **SIMONE:** He was at mine Wednesday.
> **NIA:** You're lying.
> **SIMONE:** I'm having a lovely time. Those aren't the same thing either.

Push to 75mm on Nia on the last line. Hold on her face for one beat — **she does
the arithmetic and we watch her get the answer.**

*This is the "embarrassing thing said plainly" clip. **"He was at mine Wednesday"
is the line the show exists to say out loud** and it must be delivered completely
without relish — a fact, offered helpfully.*

*"Those aren't the same thing **either**" — the "either" is load-bearing. She is
picking up Dorian's motif from Clip 3 without having heard it, which tells you
where she learned to talk. **Light stress on "either", none on "lovely".***

*Nia's tell is length and she has none here: eleven words in four lines. **She is
losing so badly she has stopped talking**, which is the inverse of her rule and the
reason it works. The next episode she loses she will not shut up.*

### CLIP 06 — "White" · 18s · 50mm · f/2.2 · locked static, then one cut
**Int. Dorian's loft, window end — night.** Same set-up, one step wider.

Nia says the thing that ends it — quiet, cold, and the nastiest line she has ever
been given:

> **NIA:** Then you can go home and wait for him. That's what you do, isn't it.
> That's the whole job.

*(beat. Simone does not flinch. Simone smiles.)*

**THE POUR.** She picks up the open bottle of red from the console — not her glass,
**the bottle** — and she pours it down the front of the white dress. Slowly.
Continuously. **Without breaking eye contact and without any expression at all.**
The way you would water a plant. It takes about three seconds and nobody in the
room moves.

She sets the bottle down upright. Then, turning to go, she sees Chi — and her whole
face opens up, warm, delighted, no edge whatsoever:

> **SIMONE:** Hey, Chi.

*(Nia turns her head. Chi does not.)*

> **CHI:** We've met.
> **NIA:** Where?
> *(one beat)*
> **CHI:** Here.

Cut to Nia. Red down the white. Hold four seconds. **Cut to black on her face, not
on Chi's.**

*⚠️ **The pour is written slow for a rendering reason, not a stylistic one** — see
the season bible. A thrown drink is a fast ballistic event the model cannot hold; a
pour is continuous, gravity-driven, in constant contact with the bottle, and
renders. **Never let a note "fix" this back into a throw.** It is also, separately,
far more frightening.*

*⚠️ **Pin the wine as a continuous stream from a held bottle**, the stain spreading
downward and outward on white fabric, wet and dark red, one long run past the hem.
Name and negate: splash, spray, droplets in the air, a glass being thrown, liquid
freezing mid-air, the dress staying clean, the stain appearing instantly.*

*Nia's line is her villain seed and the first genuinely cruel thing she has said in
seven episodes. **She is not shouting.** It lands quiet and flat and it is beneath
her, and the show does not comment on that. She earns the wine. Nobody says so.*

*⚠️ **"Here." is the most important word in the episode and it is a lie that is
technically true.** They did meet here — two years ago, when it was his apartment
and not a party. Chi is not lying about a fact, she is lying with one. **Flat,
unhurried, no hesitation before it, and she is still not looking at Nia when she
says it.** The one-beat gap before it is the only tell she gives and it must be
exactly one beat — long enough to exist, too short to accuse.*

*Hold on **Nia**, not Chi. The audience wants Chi's face and does not get it, and
that withholding is the hook into N2.*

## CONTINUITY — pinned per clip

- **Nia's dress is WHITE and it is the episode.** It must be a locked wardrobe
  element from a supplied reference, shot clean in C1, C2, C5, and stained only
  from the moment of the pour in C6. **It is the same dress in every clip.**
- **Nia's neckline never changes between clips** — never raised, never lowered,
  never swapped. This exact failure has recurred in every episode of the series.
- **Nia wears a gold ring from Ep 3 on, pinned to the same hand and finger**
  (user's ruling), and the thin gold choker, worn only if the reference leaves her
  throat bare and **never on top of fabric**.
- **Chi wears NO RING, ever.** Named object by object, including nothing on the
  fourth finger of her left hand. **This season it is a plot fact and not only a
  continuity one** — she is the other woman and a wedding ring destroys the
  premise. The gold watch is her only permitted wrist jewellery. Nails deep red.
- **Chi's hair sweeps to HER OWN RIGHT** — deep side part on the LEFT of her head,
  volume carrying across and falling down her right side. **Facing camera that
  reads as screen LEFT.** Never centre-parted, never swept to her left, never
  symmetrical, never tied back.
- **Chi's stillness is her vocabulary, not paralysis.** She turns her body toward
  whoever speaks to her, then settles and holds. In C3 she is as still as she has
  ever been; in C6 she does not turn her head at all, and that is the one time the
  stillness is a refusal rather than authority.
- **Clothes adapt to Chi's body, never the reverse** — every garment tailored
  realistically to her real full-figured proportions.
- **Everyone at this party is Black**, every guest in every frame, no exceptions.
- **Crowd rule: never more than six guests in frame**, at the edges or in deep soft
  focus, never crossing in front of the leads, never looking at the lens. They talk
  to each other — mouths moving, gesturing — but **not one intelligible word.**
- **Night key from C2 on.** Warm practicals only. Never daylight, never a pink or
  lilac sky, never blue-dominant, never coloured LED, strobes, club lighting, a DJ
  or a dance floor. C1 is the exception and is **sunset**, matching the prologue.
- **Source music only, from speakers in the room**, low and warm under the
  dialogue, never rising over a line, **no intelligible lyrics.** Measure the
  speech-band level during the lines against the level between them before asking
  the user to listen.
- **The apartment is 10B.** The lift-lobby directory panel reads 10.

⚠️ **The density check has to be run on the speaking time, not the runtime.** Raw,
this episode is 152 / 94 = **1.62 w/s**, which looks badly over-provisioned against
the 2.1 clip figure. **33 seconds of it is declared wordless action** — the four-second
silence in C3, the shoes in C4, the pour and the hold in C6. Against the 61 seconds
that actually carry dialogue it runs **2.49 w/s**, which is dense.

**That distinction is the whole point of the container rule.** Silence that is
declared, blocked and placed is not slack; slack is what a model manufactures when
a clip is longer than its lines. Every second of the 33 has an action written
against it in the script. **If a clip is trimmed, trim the action it names — never
leave the duration up and the action out.**
