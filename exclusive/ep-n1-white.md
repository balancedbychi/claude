# EXCLUSIVE — the new order
## Episode 1: "White"

**Status: PROPOSED — nothing locked, nothing spent.** Revised 16 Sep 2026. Read
`exclusive/new-order-README.md` first.

**Runtime:** 1:36 · 6 clips · 96 seconds
**Format:** One phone call, then one apartment, one continuous night.
**Logline:** Dorian says "exclusive." Nia wears white to celebrate. Ninety seconds
later another woman is barefoot in his kitchen. Chi came for Nia and leaves with
Kel's number written on a foil-wrapped plate.

---

## THE SHAPE

**The happy part is real and plays completely straight.** C1 and C2 are the warmest
material the series has had — Chi laughing on the phone, Dorian in a good mood, Nia
winning. If the audience does not want this for her, nothing after it costs
anything. **No dread anywhere in C1 or C2.**

Then two stories start in the same apartment. Nia's goes: someone arrives who
belongs here (C4), someone says the dates out loud (C5), the wine (C5). Chi's goes:
a man in a kitchen makes her a plate, and she tells him not to be right about it
(C3). **The two stories touch once**, in the last ten seconds, in a lift.

**Both women leave with something.** Nia leaves in a ruined dress. Chi leaves with a
phone number. That is the season in one frame.

---

## THE SCRIPT

Six clips, 96 seconds, one evening. Panavision Millennium XL2, Primo lenses,
eye-level. Sunset in C1, night key and warm practicals from C2. Cuts land on the
first syllable of the incoming line. **Holds allowed: the end of C1, the pour in
C5, the lift doors in C6.**

---

### CLIP 01 — "Bold" · 18s · 40mm · f/2.0 · intercut
**Int. Nia's bedroom — sunset / Int. Chi's living room — sunset.**
`Nia-Bedroom-Sunset` + `ChiChi-Living-Room`. Both phones visible, cut on the first
syllable, the Episode-1 format.

Nia at the mirror in the white dress, radiant. Chi in the corner of her sofa —
**dressed to go out, shoes on, keys and clutch beside her** — one glass, one candle.

> **NIA:** He said it. He said the word.
> **CHI** *(phone)*: What word?
> **NIA:** Exclusive. Out loud. With his mouth.
> **CHI** *(phone, a real laugh, from the chest)*: Okay. I'm happy for you.
> **NIA:** Good. Because Kel asked if you were coming.
> **CHI** *(phone)*: ...Mm.
> **NIA:** That was a very long "mm."
> **CHI** *(phone)*: What are you wearing?
> **NIA:** White.
> **CHI** *(phone)*: Bold.

Nia hangs up mid-shriek. Stay with Chi one beat. She looks at her keys. She picks
them up. Cut.

*Chi's laugh is the most important half-second in the clip and it is entirely
genuine. **Everything she says here is true.** "Bold." is dry affection with a
warning in it — white, at a party, with red wine in the room — and it is dramatic
irony for the audience only; Chi means it as a joke.*

*"That was a very long 'mm'" is the first time anyone has measured Chi's sound back
at her, and it plants Kel in one line. **Chi's "...Mm." here is warm, caught, and
half a second longer than her usual.** That half-second is the plant.*

*Chi picking up her keys is the button: she is going. No hesitation, no looking at
the clock — the inverse of the prologue's Shot 5, and deliberately so.*

### CLIP 02 — "A Dare" · 14s · 50mm · f/2.0 · two cuts
**Int. Dorian's loft, window end — night.** `Dorian-Loft-Window-Night`. Early,
before the room fills. Nia just arrived, coat over her arm. Dorian across the room,
tumbler, unhurried. **He is 6'2" and she is not.**

> **DORIAN:** Turn around.
> **NIA:** Why?
> **DORIAN:** Because I asked.
> *(she turns — slow, enjoying it, one full rotation)*
> **DORIAN:** White.
> **NIA:** Is that a problem?
> **DORIAN:** It's a statement.
> **NIA:** It's a dress.
> **DORIAN:** No.
> *(he crosses to her and stops just short — close enough that she has to look up)*
> **DORIAN:** That's a dare.

He does not touch her. The clip ends there.

*The sexual tension is the distance he closes and the contact he does not make.
**No kiss, no hand on the waist, no touch of any kind** — the model will offer one
and it must be negated. He stops a foot away and they both stay there. That is the
character: Dorian gets very close to things and does not commit to them.*

*He is warm, funny, plainly delighted by her. **No double meaning in "dare."** He is
telling the truth. He always tells the truth; he just never volunteers any.*

### CLIP 03 — "Don't Be Right About It" · 16s · 50mm · f/2.2 · two cuts
**Int. Dorian's kitchen — night.** `Dorian-Kitchen-Night`. Kel at the steel island
under the one warm pendant, sleeves pushed up, dish towel over one shoulder, the
slow cooker — the same one — lid off. His back to the door.

Chi in the doorway, clutch in hand. He does not turn around.

> **KEL** *(not turning)*: You came back.
> **CHI:** Nia asked.
> **KEL:** That the only reason?
> *(beat)*
> **CHI:** There's jollof.
> **KEL** *(turns, grinning)*: There's jollof.

He makes her a plate without asking and sets it on the island. She eats standing
up. He watches her eat. She knows he is watching.

> **CHI:** ...It's good.
> **KEL:** I know.
> **CHI:** Don't do that.
> **KEL:** Do what?
> **CHI:** Be right about it.

*"You came back" is continuity with the prologue's caterer scene and needs no
explanation — they met in this kitchen. **He turns on "There's jollof"** and not
before; the whole first exchange plays to his back.*

*⚠️ **"Don't be right about it" is Chi, of all people, telling a man to stop being
accurate.** That is the joke and the attraction: she has met someone who does what
she does, and she does not entirely like how it feels. Play it dry and warm, no
smile from her, a big one from him.*

*She eats. **Real bites, a real mouthful** — not a prop nibble. Watching her enjoy
something is the sexiest thing in the clip and the model will try to make it
dainty. Jollof: *"pronounced 'JO-loff', stress on the first syllable, never
'jaw-left', never 'joll-OFF'"* — phonetic in the prompt, checked by the user's ear,
never by a transcript.*

### CLIP 04 — "Shoes" · 13s · 40mm · f/2.2 · two cuts
**Int. hallway outside 10B → the loft — night.** `Dorian-Hallway-Night` into the
loft, **shot from the INSIDE** — so every part of the pinned door mirrors (hinges
LEFT / handle RIGHT are written from the hallway side; from inside they swap). See
`ep-the-caterer.md`, which cost three takes. Write it from the side you shoot.

The door opens. **Simone comes in without knocking and without being let in.** She
sets her bag in a particular spot. She steps out of her heels and places them
neatly side by side against the wall — **the spot she has put them a hundred
times.** She does not look down to do it.

> **SIMONE** *(to the room, bright)*: It's freezing out.
> **NIA:** I'm sorry — who—
> **SIMONE** *(already barefoot, already past her)*: Simone. You must be the new one.
> **NIA:** The new what?
> **SIMONE** *(a small, delighted, genuinely amused laugh)*: He didn't.

*The shoes are the clip. The audience understands the situation before a fact is
stated. **"You must be the new one" echoes Dorian's "You must be Chi" from the
prologue and nobody in the room notices.***

*⚠️ **Simone's register is the easiest thing in the season to get wrong.** She is
cheerful. Bright, warm, normal volume, unhurried, having a nice time. **No sneer,
no smirk, no drawl, no raised eyebrow, no hair flip, no head-tilt, no hand on hip,
no sashay** — every one of those will be offered and every one makes her small.
"He didn't." is said the way you would say "oh, bless him" about a man who forgot
to mention something at work.*

### CLIP 05 — "Since When" · 25s · 50mm → 75mm · f/2.0 · three cuts · **THE WINE**
**Int. Dorian's loft, window end — night.** The room has noticed. Guests at the
frame edges, deep soft focus, never more than six, an indistinct murmur with not
one intelligible word. **Dorian is not in this clip** — he is being discussed in the
third person twelve feet from where he is standing. **Chi is at the frame edge,
visible, still, plate in hand.** A dark open bottle of red on the console, in frame
from the first second.

> **NIA:** I think you should leave.
> **SIMONE:** I think you should ask him how long.
> **NIA:** We're exclusive.
> **SIMONE** *(pleasantly, as though correcting a booking)*: Since when?
> **NIA:** Tuesday.
> *(Simone turns her wrist and actually checks her watch. She is genuinely working
> it out.)*
> **SIMONE:** He was at mine Wednesday.
> **NIA:** You're lying.
> **SIMONE:** I'm having a lovely time. Those aren't the same thing.

Push to 75mm on Nia. She does the arithmetic and we watch her get the answer. Then
she says the thing that ends it — quiet, cold, and beneath her:

> **NIA:** Then go home and wait for him. That's the job, isn't it.

*(Simone does not flinch. Simone smiles.)*

**THE POUR.** She picks up the bottle from the console — **the bottle, not a glass**
— and pours it down the front of the white dress. Slowly. Continuously. Without
breaking eye contact and without any expression at all. The way you would water a
plant. About three seconds. Nobody in the room moves.

She sets the bottle down upright. She turns to Chi — and her whole face opens,
warm, no edge:

> **SIMONE:** Take her home.

*(Chi already has Nia's arm.)* Cut.

*⚠️ **"He was at mine Wednesday" is the line the show exists to say out loud.**
Delivered without relish — a fact, offered helpfully.*

*⚠️ **Nia's "That's the job" is her villain seed** — the first cruel thing she has
said in seven episodes. Not shouted. Flat, quiet, beneath her, and the show does not
comment. She earns the wine. Nobody says so.*

*⚠️ **The pour is slow for a rendering reason, not a stylistic one.** A thrown drink
is a fast ballistic event the model cannot hold; a pour is continuous,
gravity-driven, in contact with the bottle, and renders. Never let a note fix it
back into a throw. Pin: a continuous stream from a held bottle, the stain spreading
downward and outward on white fabric, wet and dark red, one long run past the hem.
Name and negate: splash, spray, droplets in the air, a glass thrown, liquid frozen
mid-air, the dress staying clean, the stain appearing instantly.*

*"Take her home" is Simone being kind to Chi and dismissive of Nia in three words,
and it establishes that she and Chi have now met — which is all N3 needs.*

*This clip is a **merge** of what would have been two: the argument and the pour
are one continuous piece of staging in one set, and a seam through the wine is the
worst seam available. 25s, under the cap.*

### CLIP 06 — "For the Cab" · 10s · 40mm · f/2.2 · one cut · **near-silent**
**Int. hallway — night.** `Dorian-Hallway-Night`, the same corridor, the lift doors
framed close from `Dorian-Lift-Lobby-Night` — **one place, the lift opens directly
onto the hall**, no lobby, no journey.

Chi walking Nia toward the lift, Nia in the ruined dress, Chi's hand on her arm.
Footsteps behind them. Kel — with a foil-wrapped plate.

> **KEL:** For the cab.
> **CHI** *(takes it)*: ...Mm.

He goes back. She looks down at the plate. **A phone number, written on the foil in
marker.** She looks at it for one beat. The lift arrives. The two women step in —
Nia stained, Chi holding a foil plate. The doors close on them. Hold two seconds on
the closed doors. Cut to black.

*⚠️ **The number on the foil is the season's Chi hook and it must never be legible
as digits** — rendered text garbles, and §5 says describe the absence, never the
identity. Pin: "a short line of handwriting in black marker on the foil, clearly
handwritten, clearly a phone number by its shape, **never readable as specific
digits**." The audience knows what a number on foil is.*

*Chi's "Mm" here is the third one of the episode and it means a third thing. She
does not say thank you. She does not smile. She takes the plate. **That is yes.***

*The lift doors closing on the two of them is the episode: one woman covered in
wine, one holding a plate, both going home. **Play it wide enough that both read
and no closer.***

## CONTINUITY — the lines that go in every prompt

- **Nia's white dress is a locked wardrobe element**, clean in C1, C2, C4, C5 until
  the pour, stained from the pour and identical in C6. Neckline never raised,
  lowered or swapped. Choker only if the reference leaves the throat bare, never
  on top of fabric. Gold ring, same hand, same finger.
- **Chi wears NO RING, ever** — named object by object, nothing on the fourth finger
  of her left hand. Gold watch only. Nails deep red. **Hair sweeps to HER OWN
  RIGHT** — deep side part on the LEFT of her head, falling down her right side,
  which reads as **screen LEFT** facing camera. Never centre, never her left, never
  tied back. Skin clear, luminous, every age marker negated by name (§3). Clothes
  tailored to her real full-figured proportions.
- **Chi's stillness is understated, not inert.** She turns toward whoever speaks,
  then settles. She eats in C3 and enjoys it. She takes Nia's arm in C5 without
  hesitation.
- **Everyone at the party is Black**, every guest, every frame. Never more than
  six guests in frame, edges or deep soft focus, talking to each other, not one
  intelligible word. **Source music from speakers in the room**, low, no
  intelligible lyrics, never over a line.
- **Night key from C2.** Warm practicals only. Never daylight, pink or lilac sky,
  blue-dominant, coloured LED, strobes, a DJ or a dance floor. C1 is sunset.
- **Kel is fifties**, sleeves up, towel on the shoulder; voice per the prose
  recipe, 106.7Hz / warmth 38, written against Dorian.
- **The apartment is 10B.**
