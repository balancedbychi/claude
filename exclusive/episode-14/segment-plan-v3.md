de50f37f; Tay, Zarya and the host from presets to be picked), imported as a media file, and
attached to the clip as the audio reference WITH the model's own audio generation left ON so
the track is carried in the output (the method test had it off, which is why those two clips
were silent). Off-screen replies inside a segment are generated the same way and attached; the
on-camera character reacts to them. Where a segment holds two speakers, both lines are attached
in order.
