## Where we are
Nia has just watched Zarya kiss Tay at the foot of the DJ steps. She has turned away to the bar.
First words: Zarya's "You must be Nia."

## Camera
On the floor side of the brass bar at a 45-degree angle, eye level, medium two-shot, both faces in
three-quarter. The bar runs along the LEFT and far side of frame. Clean cuts to waist-up singles in
the same direction. No zooms. The camera never crosses to the other side.

## Blocking (fixed)
- NIA at frame LEFT, leaning on the bar, facing it. ZARYA at frame RIGHT, on NIA'S OWN RIGHT,
  sliding in beside her, also facing the bar. They never swap.
- Height: Zarya a half head taller; the top of Nia's head at Zarya's eye line.
- Nia's hands: RIGHT hand (the hand nearer Zarya) flat on the bar, not steady; bag under her
  LEFT arm. Zarya: LEFT hand (nearer Nia) resting on the bar; RIGHT hand raises one finger to the
  bartender. No glasses on the bar until second 17.
- Bartender behind the counter, silent, sets down exactly two short glasses at second 17: one in
  front of Nia, one in front of Zarya. Every glass is in a hand or on the counter; nothing floats.

## Movement, second by second
0-4   Nia arrives at the bar and puts her right hand flat on it; her hand is not steady. She
      gives the bartender a small nod to order. Zarya slides in on her right, calm.
4-6   ZARYA, light, friendly: "You must be Nia." Nia's eyes stay on the bar for a half second.
6-8   Nia turns her HEAD only, a quarter turn, to Zarya: "...I'm sorry?"
8-12  Zarya makes a quarter turn to her LEFT so her body faces Nia, and offers her RIGHT hand
      from the bar side, hand vertical, thumb on top, at waist height: "Zarya. Tay's girlfriend."
      Nia looks her in the face. Nia's right hand stays flat on the bar; she does not take the
      hand yet. Her face goes blank.
12-15 Nia makes a quarter turn to her RIGHT so the two women face each other square on, the bar
      beside them, and her RIGHT hand meets Zarya's RIGHT hand in a brief correct shake, thumbs on
      top, eye to eye, Nia looking slightly up, Zarya slightly down. NIA: "Girlfriend."
15-22 Both turn back to face the bar. ZARYA, an easy story told to a friend: "Three years. I was
      here when this was a hole with no roof. Did he tell you about the space heater? He loves
      that story." At second 17 the bartender's hands set down the two glasses. Nia's hands go
      flat on the bar either side of her own glass, which stays on the counter.
22-26 Zarya's RIGHT hand picks up HER glass. ZARYA, soft, not a question: "He didn't mention me,
      did he." Nia says nothing; her eyes go down to her glass.
26-30 ZARYA, gentle, almost sympathetic: "Yeah. He does that." She taps her glass once against
      the rim of Nia's glass on the counter, and walks out of frame RIGHT into the crowd. END on
      Nia alone at the bar, eyes on her glass.
