## THE RULES THIS EPISODE ADDS

- **Geography is real and must hold.** `Dorian-Loft-Night` and
  `Dorian-Loft-Window-Night` are two ends of ONE room. The windows in
  `Dorian-Loft-Window-Night` are the SAME windows as `Dorian-Roof-Terrace-Night`,
  seen from inside — Clips 4 and 5 sit on opposite sides of one piece of glass.
- **Crowd rule: never more than SIX guests in any frame.** Kept to the frame
  edges or in deep soft focus, never crossing in front of the leads, never
  looking at the lens, mouths closed unless the clip says otherwise.
- **Night key, everywhere.** Warm practicals only. Never daylight, never sunset,
  never a pink or lilac sky, never blue-dominant, never coloured LED, strobes,
  club lighting, a DJ or a dance floor.
- **The apartment is 10B**, because the lift-lobby directory panel reads 10 and
  that panel is visible in three clips.
- **Nia's neckline.** The placket stays open below the bust, collar flat, throat
  bare, choker on skin above the fabric. This exact failure recurred all through
  Episode 1 — state it in every prompt.
- **ChiChi's gold watch** is a deliberate exception to her standing ring-free,
  bracelet-free rule, and the only wrist jewellery allowed.
- **Clothes adapt to her body, never the reverse.** Every garment tailored
  realistically to ChiChi's real full-figured proportions.
- **Kel does not know he is the punchline** and never finds out.
- **Dorian passes every test except one.** He is not a villain and must never be
  played as one — the episode depends on that.

## THE SCRIPT

Seven clips, ~70 seconds, 22:00 → 00:00, same night as Episode 1 ends.
Panavision Millennium XL2, Primo lenses, eye-level throughout. Dialogue runs as
one unbroken overlapping run, cuts land on the first syllable of the incoming
line. Exactly three holds are allowed: end of Clip 4, end of Clip 5, all of 7.

Full formatted script with direction notes:
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/3a3788b2-5c51-460c-9236-d5d138aaaa3c

### Clip 01 — "Ten" · 40mm · f/2.0 · one cut
**Int. lift lobby — night.** ChiChi steps out of the lift alone, walks the length
of the lobby, stops at the door. Muffled party behind it. She holds one full beat
— long enough that we understand she considered leaving. Then knocks. Nia in the
doorway, lit from behind.

> NIA: "You came."
> CHICHI: "I said I would."
> NIA: "You said midnight."
> CHICHI: "I still say midnight."

Nia takes her wrist and pulls her in. Hold one second on the empty hallway.
*The deliberate inverse of Episode 1's last shot — there she walked out of frame
in her own apartment; here she walks into someone else's. Keep the knock late.
The hesitation is the performance.*

### Clip 02 — "All of it good" · 40mm · f/2.0 · two cuts
**Int. loft — night.** Dorian crosses to meet them, unhurried, whiskey in hand.

> DORIAN: "You must be Chi. I've heard a lot."
> CHICHI: "Have you."
> DORIAN: "All of it good."
> CHICHI: "That's because she likes you." *(not a compliment, not an insult)*
> DORIAN: "I'll take it." *(a real laugh, from the chest)*

End on Nia's face, thrilled. She has read this as a win.
*Dorian's laugh is the most important half-second in the clip. It has to be
likeable.*

### Clip 03 — "The Caterer" · 50mm · f/2.2 · two cuts
**Int. kitchen — night.** Kel at the steel island under the pendants, stirring
the slow cooker, dish towel over one shoulder.

> CHICHI: "Are you the caterer?"
> KEL: "I'm his cousin."
> CHICHI: "Mm."
> KEL: "There's jollof, though."
> *(one beat — she looks at the slow cooker, then back at him)*
> CHICHI: "Then you're the caterer."

End on the slow cooker, lid off, steam under the one warm pendant.
*The literal payoff of her Episode 1 line and the only outright comedy in the
episode. Kel being a generation older is what makes it land. Play it generous —
she likes him immediately and he never finds out he is the punchline. "Mm." is
her established sound; keep it identical.*

### Clip 04 — "My friend Nia" · 75mm → 50mm · f/2.0 · two cuts · HOLDS IN SILENCE
**Int. loft, window end — night.** ChiChi three steps up the staircase, out of
the flow of the room. Long lens from her eyeline: Dorian at the window wall with
three guests, Nia beside him, her hand not quite touching his back. He turns and
gestures to her without thinking about it at all.

> DORIAN: "Guys — this is my friend Nia."
> *(one beat — her smile does not drop, it adjusts)*
> NIA: "Hi."

Cut to ChiChi on the stairs, alone above the room, holding a paper plate. She has
heard it. She does not react. She files it away and takes a drink. Hold on her.

***This clip is the episode.*** Two failure modes to prompt hard against: Nia's
smile COLLAPSING — it must hold then correct by about five percent, nothing more
— and ChiChi REACTING. No smirk, no eye-roll, no glance to camera. The audience
does the work.

### Clip 05 — "Twelve" · 50mm · f/2.0 · slow push · ENDS IN SILENCE
**Ext. roof terrace — night.** The two of them alone, party a warm blur through
the glass. Almost imperceptible push across the whole clip.

> NIA: "Say it."
> CHICHI: "I don't have anything to say."
> NIA: "You have a whole paragraph."
> CHICHI: "I have a question."
> NIA: "Fine."
> CHICHI: "What did he call you?"
> *(Nia turns to the city. She does not answer that.)*
> NIA: "It's eleven days, Chi."
> CHICHI: "Twelve."
> *(Nia's head comes round slowly.)*
> CHICHI: "I'm not counting to win. I'm counting because you stopped."

Hold on Nia, silent, caught. She has nothing. The clip ends on her.
*The mirror of Episode 1's wordless beat — there the silence was ChiChi's, here
it is Nia's. "Twelve" lands almost gently; ChiChi has been counting since the
call and is not proud of it. Do not let the model add a Nia reply.*

### Clip 06 — "Midnight" · 40mm · f/2.0 · two cuts
**Int. entry — night.** ChiChi at the door, turning her wrist to look at the gold
watch — the only time she checks it on camera. Nia behind her, barefoot, heels in
one hand.

> NIA: "Stay."
> CHICHI: "You asked me to be in the room. I was in the room."
> NIA: "Chi—"
> CHICHI: "Midnight."

No hug. She touches Nia's arm once and goes, away down the bronze lobby, getting
smaller. Nia watches the whole way, then turns back to the party and closes the
door. The lobby is empty.
*Nia staying is the whole ending. ChiChi does not make her see it — no ultimatum,
no last word, no "call me." The door closing on Nia's side is the beat.*

### Clip 07 — "Down" · 50mm · f/2.2 · locked static · NO DIALOGUE
**Int. lift lobby — night.** Wide, static, deep. ChiChi alone at the nearest bay,
small against the receding row of bronze doors. She presses the call button. While
she waits she takes out her phone and looks at the screen. There is nothing on it.
She puts it away. The lift arrives, she steps in, the doors close. Hold three
seconds on nothing.
*Bookend of Episode 1's empty sectional. Ep 1: alone in a room she had not chosen
to be alone in. Ep 2: alone in a corridor she chose. Play it wide and let her be
small — the one shot that should not be a close-up. No music, no score, no chime,
no ambience beyond a low building hum. The audio drop when the door shuts in Clip
6 carries all the way through.*

**Optional 07B (~4s, hook for Ep 3):** back on the terrace, Nia alone, phone in
her hand, not calling anyone. No dialogue.

## SET GAP FOUND IN THE SHOOT — THE HALLWAY

**`Dorian-Lift-Lobby-Night` contains no apartment door.** It is a corridor of
bronze lift bays and nothing else. The script asks ChiChi to stop at "the door
past the last bay" and knock — so the first take had her knocking on a lift.
Unusable, 37.5 credits.

The user supplied a hallway reference in the same batch as the apartment images;
it was never made into an element. A separate `Dorian-Hallway-Night` set is
required, carrying the door to 10B.

**WARNING — the first version of this fix was ALSO wrong, and the wrong version
shot three takes.** It treated the lobby and the hallway as two spaces with a walk
between them: ChiChi steps out of the lift into a lobby, crosses it, and moves
into a separate hallway. The user's verdict: *"she's in the elevator lobby and
walking out into the hallways which MAKES NO SENSE."* Nobody asked for two rooms.
The user said "10 is the hallway" and a two-set journey was invented on top of it.

**THE GEOGRAPHY, AS THE USER ACTUALLY DESCRIBES IT:**

> She is coming out of the elevator. She is walking down the hall. She knocks on
> the door. Nia opens the door from the side where the handle is, and that's where
> the dialogue begins.

**The lift opens DIRECTLY ONTO the hallway. It is ONE place.** You step out of the
lift and you are already in the hall — one step, nothing in between. There is no
lobby, no anteroom, no landing, no corridor of multiple lift bays, and no journey
from one space into another. Frame the opening TIGHT on a single pair of bronze
lift doors so no wide lobby ever establishes.

`Dorian-Lift-Lobby-Night` supplies the lift doors only, framed close. It must
never be established as a room in its own right in Clip 1.

**Continuity from the previous clips: the gold metallic clutch is in ChiChi's hand
from the very first frame** — when the lift doors open, through the walk, and at
the apartment door. The user flagged its absence specifically.

**What it changes:**

| Clip | Geography |
|---|---|
| 01 | ONE corridor. Lift doors open → she is in the hallway → walk → knock → doorway |
| 06 | the same hallway for the goodbye at the door, then the same corridor as she leaves |
| 07 | the same corridor, waiting at the lift doors — no apartment door needed |

**Check that a set actually contains the thing the action needs.** A locked plate
proves what a room looks like, not that it holds the door, window or furniture
the script asks an actor to use. Read the blocking against the set spec before
generating, not after.

**And do not invent structure the user never asked for.** The two-set walk was not
in any note, any reference or any script line — it was added while "fixing" the
missing door, and then written into this file as if it were canon, where it
survived three takes and the user's own correction. When a reference arrives, use
it for what it shows. Adding geography around it is set design, and section 2
forbids inventing that mid-build.

## THE DOOR TO 10B — ITS HARDWARE

The door appears in Clips 1 and 6 and has to behave the same way both times.
The first hallway take drew it hinged left, knob right, and then opened it from
the left with the knob still on the right.

**Pinned, in both prompts, byte-identical:**

- Hinges on the **LEFT** edge as we look at it.
- Handle, lock and knob on the **RIGHT** edge.
- They are **never on the same side** — that is physically impossible.
- It opens **inward**, pivoting on the left hinged edge, so the gap opens on the
  **right**.
- Nia holds the handle at the right-hand edge when she opens it.

## CLIP 1 — THE LINE THAT WENT TO THE WRONG MOUTH

ChiChi delivered Nia's line **"You said midnight."** It is Nia's line and the
reply to it — ChiChi's "I still say midnight" — only works if a different woman
says each one.

**It is in BOTH takes, not just the 1080p one.** Measured per line, the pattern is
identical in the 480p take (`545524d2`) and the 1080p take (`3dbf92d0`): line 3
sits closer to the ChiChi lines than to the Nia line in both. The resolution
change did not cause this. It was there the first time and the verification pass
missed it, because pitch was averaged per *scripted* speaker instead of per line.

**Why this shot is prone to it.** Four lines, one cut, and a doorway two-shot in
which the prompt puts Nia **lit from behind**. A backlit face is the hardest thing
for the model to bind a line to, and lines 3 and 4 both end on the word
"midnight" — adjacent lines sharing a word is exactly where attribution slips.

**The fix, one variable:** every line tagged with its own speaker, the other
woman's mouth explicitly closed through it, and the wrong answer named outright.
Nia's backlight is NOT being changed in the same take — if per-line tagging does
not hold it, relighting her face is the next single variable to try.

| Take | Res | Cost | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| first | 480p | 37.5 | knocked on a lift — no hallway set |
| `545524d2` | 480p | 37.5 | characters right, door hinge wrong, **line 3 misattributed** |
| `3dbf92d0` | 1080p | 135 | door pinned, resolution up, **line 3 still misattributed** |
| `88e888cf` | 480p | 37.5 | per-line tagging — **line 3 STILL misattributed** |

**Per-line tagging did not fix it.** Take 3 named the speaker on every line, closed
the other woman's mouth through it, and named the wrong answer outright
("NEVER give ChiChi the line *You said midnight*"). Line 3 still went to ChiChi.
Prompt-level attribution is not the lever.

The measurement in take 3 is the cleanest of the three, because the two voices
separated further apart than in either earlier take:

| line | scripted | f0 median |
|---|---|---|
| L1 "You came" | NIA | 239.0 Hz |
| L2 "I said I would" | CHICHI | 153.1 Hz |
| **L3 "You said midnight"** | **NIA** | **190.5 Hz** |
| L4 "I still say midnight" | CHICHI | 169.3 Hz |

L3 sits with the ChiChi lines, not with Nia's, and the within-clip cluster agrees
(L3 vs L1 0.605; L3 vs L2/L4 0.713/0.779; same-speaker control 0.938).

**Good news buried in the same take:** ChiChi read **153.1 / 169.3 Hz** against
canon's 160.0 — the closest to canon she has measured in this episode. The 1080p
take had her at 193.9 / 222.2. The pinned recipe is working.

**One regression:** a stray speech-shaped sound at **2.52s**, inside the silent
walk, at 1.94x ambience. The earlier takes had clean silence to 9.3s.

**The pattern across all three takes: Nia gets line 1, then ChiChi takes 2, 3 and
4.** The model settles on one face after the first exchange. That points at what
the camera is doing, not at the wording — which is the next thing to establish,
and it costs nothing to ask.
