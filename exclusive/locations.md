## DORIAN'S LOFT — the full fact sheet

**Moved here from `ep-n1-white.md` because the loft appears in n1, n4 and "The
Caterer".** The episode file keeps the working history of how it was resolved; this is
the standing reference.

### Zones in order along the long axis, from the front door

1. **ENTRY** — front door, shoe wall, five black iron hooks. `9ced01df`
2. **STAIRCASE END** — black steel stair up the **RIGHT-hand** wall, **GREEN leather**
   three-seat beneath it, pale dusty-pink squiggle table, cream tubular chairs and the
   gallery wall **LEFT**, yellow-and-white globe pendant. `1a162d9b`
3. **KITCHEN** — open to the room, no dividing wall. Brushed stainless island
   free-standing, three black dome pendants in a row, matte black timber-grain

4. **WINDOW END** — full-height black-framed gridded steel windows across the end wall.
   **LOW DARK** sofa, console with the warm lamp and the one open bottle, potted plant.
   Sparse and quiet. `0707edcb`
5. **ROOF TERRACE**, beyond the glass. `a6c1e4ca`

Above zones 1–2, the **MEZZANINE** — an upper landing that runs back over the near half
and stops; the far half is full height. `48edf8ab`

### The four rulings

| | |
|---|---|
| **The mezzanine** | is what makes the locked descriptions agree. ### The glass map — glass exists in exactly four places

1. the **far end window wall** · 2. the **mezzanine's own window** · 3. the **terrace
edge railing** · 4. the kitchen's **interior glazed screen**.

**Every other surface is solid.** The front door is solid matte black steel with no
vision panel; its wall is solid concrete; every interior wall is board-marked concrete.

**⚠️ Item 4 is partial.** `de2063f2` places that screen "to the RIGHT" of its own
framing, which does not locate it in the room. Name it explicitly in kitchen prompts and
never negate glazing wholesale in a wide.

### ⚠️ Three loft elements describe a DIFFERENT episode's clips

`1a162d9b`, `0707edcb` and the retired `dc0d5423` all say things like *"Dorian
introduces Nia to the group in Clip 4"* and *"Clip 5 and Clip 4 sit on opposite sides of

## THE STANDING RULES THIS AUDIT PRODUCED

1. **Count usage before resolving a conflict.** An element with no footage is not a
   competing authority, it is an unexploded one, and retiring it is free.
2. **A pair of elements for one room at two hours must share an identical stage map.**
   Nia's bedroom is the model. Only the hour and the light change.
3. **"Supersedes" in an element description is not authority.** Three elements in this
   workspace claim it and two of the claims are wrong. Descriptions cannot be edited, so
   that text will keep looking authoritative forever. **This table is the authority.**
4. **Name the establishment, name the sofa, name the surface.** Wherever two things in
   this series share a category, the only thing keeping them apart is that each one is
   written down.
